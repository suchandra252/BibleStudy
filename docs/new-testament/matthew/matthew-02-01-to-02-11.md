### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 2:1–2</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> 1 Now after Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea in the days of Herod the king, <span style="background-color:#FFE4E1; padding:1px 4px; border-radius:3px;">magi from the east</span> arrived in Jerusalem, saying,<br>
> 2 “Where is He who has been born <span style="background-color:#FFE4E1; padding:1px 4px; border-radius:3px;">King of the Jews</span>? For we saw His star in the east and have come to <span style="background-color:#FFE4E1; padding:1px 4px; border-radius:3px;">worship Him</span>.”
> </span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">The Magi from the East</span>

After Jesus was born in Bethlehem, magi (wise men) from the East arrived in Jerusalem.  

They were Gentiles — possibly from Persia or Babylon. They had seen a special star and understood it as a sign that a great King had been born. Their purpose was clear: **they had come to worship Him**.

It is significant that the first people in Matthew’s Gospel who actively seek Jesus in order to worship Him are Gentiles, not Jews. God was already opening the door to the nations.

The star is a picture of the prophetic word of God that leads seekers to Christ (see 2 Peter 1:19).

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 2:3–6</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> 3 When Herod the king heard this, he was <span style="background-color:#FFE4E1; padding:1px 4px; border-radius:3px;">troubled</span>, and all Jerusalem with him.<br>
> 4 Gathering together all the chief priests and scribes of the people, he inquired of them where the Messiah was to be born.<br>
> 5 They said to him, “In Bethlehem of Judea; for this is what has been written by the prophet:<br>
> 6 ‘AND YOU, BETHLEHEM, LAND OF JUDAH, ARE BY NO MEANS LEAST AMONG THE LEADERS OF JUDAH; FOR OUT OF YOU SHALL COME FORTH A RULER WHO WILL SHEPHERD MY PEOPLE ISRAEL.’”
> </span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">Herod and the Religious Leaders</span>

When Herod heard that a new “King of the Jews” had been born, he was deeply troubled. All Jerusalem was troubled with him.  

Herod gathered the chief priests and scribes. They correctly quoted Micah 5:2 and pointed to Bethlehem. They had the knowledge of Scripture, but they had no living revelation and no desire to go and worship the Messiah themselves.

This is a picture of a religious system that possesses the Bible yet remains undisturbed and indifferent when Christ is near.

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #F1C40F; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">🔑 Key Insight</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#5D4E00;">
> It is possible to know the correct Scriptures and still have no living interest in the Person they point to.  
> The chief priests and scribes knew Micah 5:2 perfectly, yet none of them went to Bethlehem to worship the newborn King.  
> Knowledge without revelation produces only indifference.
> </span>
> </div>

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 2:7–8</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> 7 Then Herod secretly called the magi and determined from them the exact time the star appeared.<br>
> 8 And he sent them to Bethlehem and said, “Go and search carefully for the Child; and when you have found Him, report to me, so that I too may come and worship Him.”
> </span>

Herod pretended interest and spoke of worship, but his real intention was murder.  

He represents every religious or political system that feels threatened by the true King and tries to use spiritual language to protect its own position.

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 2:9–11</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> 9 After hearing the king, they went their way; and the star, which they had seen in the east, went on before them until it came and stood over the place where the Child was.<br>
> 10 When they saw the star, they rejoiced exceedingly with great joy.<br>
> 11 After coming into the house they saw the Child with Mary His mother; and they fell to the ground and <span style="background-color:#FFE4E1; padding:1px 4px; border-radius:3px;">worshiped Him</span>. Then, opening their treasures, they presented to Him gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh.
> </span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">The Joy of Finding Jesus</span>

When the star reappeared and led them to the exact place, the magi “rejoiced exceedingly with great joy.”  

They entered the house, saw the Child with His mother Mary, and **fell down and worshiped Him** (not Mary).  

Then they opened their treasures and presented three gifts:

- **Gold** – for a King  
- **Frankincense** – for a Priest  
- **Myrrh** – pointing to His suffering and death  

They did not stop at the star or at the religious experts in Jerusalem. They followed the revelation until they found the Person Himself and worshiped Him.

> <div style="background-color:#D4EDDA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #28A745; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#155724; font-weight:bold;">✅ Important Truth</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#0B3D1B;">
> The star (a picture of God’s Word) is meant to lead us to Jesus Himself, not merely to doctrines or religious systems.  
> True seekers do not stop at knowledge or at human experts. They continue until they find the living Person and fall down to worship Him.
> </span>
> </div>

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Main Lessons from This Passage</span>

1. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">God reveals Himself to sincere seekers</span>  
   The magi had no full Bible, yet God gave them enough light to find the King.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 2:1-2 · Jeremiah 29:13 · Acts 10:1-4</span>

2. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Religious knowledge without revelation is empty</span>  
   The chief priests and scribes knew the correct Scripture but had no desire to worship the Messiah.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 2:4-6 · John 5:39-40 · 2 Timothy 3:7</span>

3. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">The star leads only to Jesus</span>  
   God’s Word is given to bring us to the Person of Christ, not to stop at doctrines or human teachers.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 2:9-10 · 2 Peter 1:19 · John 5:39</span>

4. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">True worship is costly and personal</span>  
   The magi fell down, opened their treasures, and gave their best. Worship always costs something.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 2:11 · Romans 12:1 · 2 Samuel 24:24</span>

5. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Do not lean on your own understanding</span>  
   The magi almost missed the way by consulting Herod and the religious experts. We must trust the Holy Spirit’s guidance more than human reasoning.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Proverbs 3:5-6 · Matthew 2:9 · 1 Corinthians 2:14</span>
