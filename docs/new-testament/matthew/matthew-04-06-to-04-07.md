### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 4:5–6</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> 5 Then the devil took Him into the holy city and had Him stand on the pinnacle of the temple,<br>
> 6 and said to Him, “If You are the Son of God, throw Yourself down; for it is written, ‘He will command His angels concerning You’; and ‘On their hands they will bear You up, so that You will not strike Your foot against a stone.’”
> </span>

<img src="https://media.freebibleimages.org/stories/FB_GNPI_014_Jesus_Temptation/overview-thumbnails/04_FB_GNPI_014_Jesus_Temptation_Thumbnail.jpg?1725894120" width="500">
#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">The Second Temptation – Presumption</span>

The devil took Jesus from the wilderness into the holy city and set Him on the pinnacle of the temple. Temptation does not come only in difficult places (the wilderness). It also comes in religious settings and places that appear spiritual.

Once again the devil began with “If You are the Son of God…” trying to create doubt about the Father’s clear declaration. Then he quoted Scripture — Psalm 91:11-12 — but only partially. He left out the vital context that God’s protection is for those who dwell in the secret place of the Most High and walk in His ways (Psalm 91:1).

The temptation was to do something spectacular without any leading from the Father — to jump and force God to protect Him. This would have been acting in presumption, seeking personal honour, and testing God. It was an attempt to use Scripture to justify a step that God had never commanded.

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">Satan’s Partial Use of Scripture</span>

The devil knows the Bible and can quote it. His method is often to take a verse out of context and present it as a reason for doing something God has not led us to do. Many believers fall into deception because they know only isolated verses and do not know the whole counsel of God.

Jesus was being tempted to perform a public miracle to prove His identity and gain recognition as the Messiah. The path of the cross and quiet obedience was far harder than a spectacular display that would impress the crowds.

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #F1C40F; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">🔑 Key Insight</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#5D4E00;">
> • Temptation comes not only in the wilderness but also in religious and “holy” places.<br>
> • The devil quotes Scripture, but only partially and out of context.<br>
> • Presumption is taking a step God has not commanded and then claiming His promises for protection.<br>
> • Seeking spectacular signs for personal recognition is a serious temptation even for spiritual people.<br>
> • Angels protect those who walk in God’s ways — not those who test Him.
> </span>
> </div>

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 4:7</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> Jesus said to him, “On the other hand, it is written, ‘You shall not put the Lord your God to the test.’”
> </span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">“It Is Also Written”</span>

Jesus answered with perfect balance: “On the other hand, it is written…” (or “It is also written”). He did not argue about the verse the devil quoted. He simply brought another Scripture that put the first one in its proper context — Deuteronomy 6:16: “You shall not put the Lord your God to the test.”

This is the way to overcome deception. One verse alone can be twisted. The whole of Scripture balances and protects us. When the devil presents a partial truth, the Holy Spirit can bring to our remembrance the balancing truth.

Temptation is not sin. Jesus was tempted in every point exactly as we are, yet without sin (Hebrews 4:15). Temptation becomes sin only when the lust in our flesh is allowed to conceive and give birth to action (James 1:14-15). Jesus never allowed any wrong desire to take root. He remained completely pure because He had emptied Himself of divine privileges and lived as a man wholly dependent on the Father and the written Word.

> <div style="background-color:#D4EDDA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #28A745; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#155724; font-weight:bold;">✅ Important Truth</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#0B3D1B;">
> • Jesus answered partial Scripture with balancing Scripture: “It is also written.”<br>
> • Knowing the whole counsel of God protects us from deception.<br>
> • We must never take a step God has not commanded and then claim His promises.<br>
> • Temptation itself is not sin; sin begins when lust is allowed to conceive.<br>
> • Jesus overcame as a man who lived by every word of God and refused to test the Father.
> </span>
> </div>

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Main Lessons from This Passage</span>

1. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Temptation comes in religious settings too</span>  
   The devil does not only attack in the wilderness; he also attacks in the “holy city.”  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 4:5 · 2 Corinthians 11:14 · 1 Peter 5:8</span>

2. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Beware of partial Scripture</span>  
   The devil quotes the Bible but leaves out the context. We need the whole counsel of God.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 4:6 · Acts 20:27 · 2 Timothy 3:16-17</span>

3. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Never put God to the test</span>  
   Taking a step without God’s leading and then expecting Him to protect us is presumption.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 4:7 · Deuteronomy 6:16 · Psalm 91:1-2</span>

4. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Answer with “It is also written”</span>  
   Balance one Scripture with another under the guidance of the Holy Spirit.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 4:7 · John 16:13 · Psalm 119:160</span>

5. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Temptation is not sin</span>  
   Jesus was tempted in every point like us, yet without sin. Sin begins only when lust is allowed to conceive.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Hebrews 4:15 · James 1:14-15 · 1 Corinthians 10:13</span>

> Live under the protection of the whole counsel of God,  
> and you will not put the Lord your God to the test.
