### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 3:5–6</span>

<img src="https://i.pinimg.com/736x/a5/76/9e/a5769e71d1ddabe14dd805846b595b18.jpg" width="500">
#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> 5 Then Jerusalem was going out to him, and all Judea and all the district around the Jordan;<br>
> 6 and they were being baptized by him in the Jordan River, as they <span style="background-color:#FFE4E1; padding:1px 4px; border-radius:3px;">confessed their sins</span>.
> </span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">People Drawn Without Advertising</span>

All of Jerusalem, Judea, and the region around the Jordan went out to John in the wilderness. There was no advertising programme. People took the trouble to travel into the wilderness because there was a man anointed with God’s Spirit who had the word of God.

Wherever there is a man truly anointed by the Holy Spirit and carrying the pure word of God, hungry hearts will still come — even if religious leaders warn against him, and even if he is preaching in the wilderness.

John did not baptize people simply to increase numbers or keep statistics. He baptized only those who confessed their sins. This is a clear mark of a true man of God: he is not interested in numbers, but in genuine repentance.

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #F1C40F; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">🔑 Key Insight</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#5D4E00;">
> • When a man is anointed with God’s Spirit and carries His word, people will come even without any advertising.<br>
> • Hungry hearts will still seek him out, even if religious leaders oppose him.<br>
> • John was not interested in numbers or statistics — only in genuine confession of sin and repentance.<br>
> • This remains a clear mark of a true servant of God.
> </span>
> </div>

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 3:7–10</span>

<img src="https://i.pinimg.com/736x/59/5b/99/595b996f882f1765d6cdb6bb85683b21.jpg" width="500">
#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> 7 But when he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming for baptism, he said to them, “You brood of vipers, who warned you to flee from the wrath to come?<br>
> 8 Therefore <span style="background-color:#FFE4E1; padding:1px 4px; border-radius:3px;">bear fruit in keeping with repentance</span>;<br>
> 9 and do not suppose that you can say to yourselves, ‘We have Abraham for our father’; for I say to you that from these stones God is able to raise up children to Abraham.<br>
> 10 The <span style="background-color:#FFE4E1; padding:1px 4px; border-radius:3px;">axe is already laid at the root of the trees</span>; therefore every tree that does not bear good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.
> </span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">Fruit That Matches Repentance</span>

When John saw many Pharisees and Sadducees coming for baptism, he did not welcome them with open arms. He called them a “brood of vipers” and asked who had warned them to flee from the coming wrath.

He refused to baptize them without seeing clear evidence of repentance. Verbal claims were not enough. He told them to bring forth fruit in keeping with repentance. Ancestry (being children of Abraham) or religious heritage means nothing without a genuine spiritual experience and a new birth. God is able to raise up children for Abraham even from stones.

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">The Axe at the Root</span>

John declared that the axe is already laid at the root of the trees. Every tree that does not bear good fruit will be cut down and thrown into the fire.

True repentance is not merely removing bad fruits (anger, bitterness, lust, love of money, jealousy, unforgiveness). The root itself — the self-centred, earth-seeking nature inherited from Adam — must be dealt with. It is not enough to cut off a few bad branches. The axe must go to the root so that a new tree with a new nature is formed — a tree that cannot produce bad fruit.

This is the beginning of the New Covenant gospel: repentance that allows the axe to be laid at the root of the old self-life.

> <div style="background-color:#D4EDDA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #28A745; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#155724; font-weight:bold;">✅ Important Truth</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#0B3D1B;">
> • John refused to baptize those who showed no fruit of repentance.<br>
> • Religious heritage or ancestry means nothing without genuine new birth.<br>
> • True repentance is not just removing bad fruits — the axe must be laid at the root of the self-centred life.<br>
> • The old nature must be dealt with so that a new tree with a new nature is formed.<br>
> • This is the foundation of the New Covenant message of repentance.
> </span>
> </div>

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 3:11–12</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> 11 “As for me, I baptize you with water for repentance, but He who is coming after me is mightier than I, and I am not fit to remove His sandals; He will baptize you with the <span style="background-color:#FFE4E1; padding:1px 4px; border-radius:3px;">Holy Spirit and fire</span>.<br>
> 12 His winnowing fork is in His hand, and He will thoroughly clear His threshing floor; and He will gather His wheat into the barn, but He will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire.”
> </span>

<img src="https://i0.wp.com/spiritwordcross.blog/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/294807286_10221368615260406_4640788767688642362_n.jpg?ssl=1" width="500">
#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">Baptism with the Holy Spirit and Fire</span>

John baptized with water for repentance. But the One coming after him — Jesus — is far greater. John said he was not even fit to remove His sandals. This shows John’s deep humility. He submitted only to Jesus, not to religious leaders, kings, or any human authority.

Jesus will baptize with the Holy Spirit and fire. This is the second great promise of the New Testament (the first being that He will save His people from their sins — Matthew 1:21). The baptism of the Holy Spirit and fire is the progressive work of the Spirit burning up the lusts of the flesh throughout a believer’s life, so that the divine nature increasingly replaces the old sinful nature.

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">The Thorough Cleansing</span>

Jesus holds the winnowing fork in His hand. He will thoroughly clean His threshing floor (our lives). He will gather the wheat into the barn and burn the chaff (the flesh and its lusts) with unquenchable fire. This cleansing work continues throughout the Christian life as we allow the Holy Spirit to deal with every area of the flesh.

> <div style="background-color:#D4EDDA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #28A745; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#155724; font-weight:bold;">✅ Important Truth</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#0B3D1B;">
> • John baptized with water for repentance; Jesus baptizes with the Holy Spirit and fire.<br>
> • This is the second foundational promise of the New Testament.<br>
> • The baptism of fire is the progressive burning up of the flesh so that the divine nature can replace the old nature.<br>
> • Jesus thoroughly cleanses His threshing floor — our lives — gathering the wheat and burning the chaff.<br>
> • True New Covenant life involves both repentance (axe at the root) and the ongoing baptism of the Holy Spirit and fire.
> </span>
> </div>

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Main Lessons from This Passage</span>

1. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">True ministry needs no advertising</span>  
   When a man is anointed by God, hungry hearts will still come.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 3:5 · John 3:27 · Acts 5:14</span>

2. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Baptism requires fruit of repentance</span>  
   Verbal claims are not enough. There must be clear evidence of a changed life.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 3:8 · Acts 26:20 · Luke 3:8</span>

3. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">The axe must go to the root</span>  
   True repentance deals with the self-centred nature, not just external sins.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 3:10 · Romans 6:6 · Galatians 2:20</span>

4. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Jesus baptizes with the Holy Spirit and fire</span>  
   This is the ongoing work of purifying the believer and replacing the old nature with the divine nature.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 3:11 · Acts 1:5 · 2 Peter 1:4</span>

5. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Jesus thoroughly cleanses His people</span>  
   He will not leave the chaff. He will complete the purifying work.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 3:12 · Malachi 3:2-3 · 1 Thessalonians 5:23</span>
