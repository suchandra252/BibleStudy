# <span style="color:#8B0000;">📖 Matthew 1:18–25</span>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 1. The Holy Spirit Produces the Life of Jesus</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text (Matthew 1:18)</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> Now the birth of Jesus Christ was as follows: when His mother Mary had been betrothed to Joseph, before they came together she was found to be with child by the Holy Spirit.
> </span>

In verse 18 we read that the <span style="background-color:#FFE4E1; padding:1px 4px; border-radius:3px;"><b>Holy Spirit produced life</b></span> in Mary’s womb.

In the same way, the Holy Spirit comes to produce the life of Jesus in every believer. He takes the tiny seed of new life and slowly shapes us into the image of Christ, day by day.

Mary may not have felt any special change in her body on the very first day Jesus was conceived. Only as the weeks passed did she realise the child was growing, and later others also recognised that she was pregnant.

It is often the same with us. At the moment the Holy Spirit fills us we may not feel anything dramatic. But as days go by, both we and the people around us begin to see the change.

Under the new covenant the work of the Holy Spirit can sometimes bring misunderstanding or humiliation, just as it did for Mary.

The Holy Spirit has no physical body, yet He can do supernatural things that human minds cannot fully explain. His <u>main work</u>, however, is not to heal bodies or make us rich. His primary purpose is to form the character and life of Jesus inside us and to establish the testimony of Jesus.

In some cases He does work supernaturally in the body (the virgin birth of Jesus, the healing of the woman with the twelve-year flow of blood, the healing of the lame, the raising of the dead). But these are secondary.

The Holy Spirit needs men and women who offer their bodies as pure living sacrifices to the Lord.

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 2. “You Shall Call His Name Jesus” (Matthew 1:21)</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> She will bear a Son; and you shall call His name <span style="background-color:#FFE4E1; padding:1px 4px; border-radius:3px;"><b>Jesus</b></span>, for He will save His people from their sins.
> </span>

The angel said the Son would be born and that He would save His people from their sins; therefore His name would be Jesus.

**Key applications:**

- God already knew your gender, your colour, your height, your abilities and your limitations before you were born. <u>Be content</u> with the way He made you.
- Do not try to become someone else.
- God has a specific plan for your life and knows your future completely.
- He knew your name even before you were born.

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 3. Jesus and Immanuel – Two Names, One Person</span>

| Verse | Name | Meaning |
|-------|------|---------|
| Matthew 1:21 | <b>Jesus</b> | “He will save His people from their sins” |
| Matthew 1:23 | <b>Immanuel</b> | “God with us” |

These are not two different people or two competing names. They <u>complement</u> each other.

Because God is with us (Immanuel), He is able to save us from our sins (Jesus).  
Because He saves us from our sins, He is able to remain with us forever.

**Immanuel applies to the whole Trinity:**

- This name is given to Jesus.
- It is also true of the <b>Holy Spirit</b>, because He is God who lives with us and in us always.
- It is equally true of the <b>Father</b>, who has promised never to leave us or forsake us.

> The same “God with us” is true of the Father and the Holy Spirit.

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 4. When Did the Angels Appear to Mary and Joseph?</span>

The angels did <b>not</b> appear on the same day.

1. First the angel appeared to Mary (<b>Luke 1:26–38</b>).
2. Some time later Joseph discovered that Mary was pregnant (<b>Matthew 1:18</b>). By this time the pregnancy was already visible.
3. Only then did the angel appear to Joseph in a dream (<b>Matthew 1:20</b>).

It is possible that two months or more had passed before Joseph became aware of the situation.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 5. Joseph’s Immediate Obedience (Matthew 1:24)</span>

As soon as Joseph woke from the dream:

- He obeyed God <u>at once</u>.
- He took Mary as his wife without delay.
- He trusted the message he had received and did not wait for further confirmation or human approval.

Joseph had been planning to divorce Mary quietly. After the dream he completely changed course and **married her the same day**. People around him may have questioned his decision:  
<span style="color:#922B21; font-style:italic;">“You were going to break the engagement – why are you marrying her now?”</span>

Joseph paid no attention to their opinions or to his own earlier reasoning. He acted solely on the revelation he had received.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 6. Mary’s Quiet Submission</span>

Mary chose to accept lifelong misunderstanding and a bad reputation rather than defend herself.

She did not try to justify herself before people or even before Joseph. She simply waited for God to clear her name in His own time. She was not shaken by circumstances or by the opinions of others. Her faith in God remained <b>unshakable</b>.

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ 7. Key Lessons</span>

1. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">God’s work is supernatural and perfect</span>
2. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">The Holy Spirit can work fully only when we surrender</span>
3. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">God has a unique plan for every life</span>
4. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Do not compare yourself with others – accept God’s design for you</span>
5. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">True obedience means acting immediately on what God has shown you</span>
6. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Jesus is both Saviour (Jesus) and God with us (Immanuel)</span>
