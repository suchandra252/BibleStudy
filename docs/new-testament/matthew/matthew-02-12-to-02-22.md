### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 2:12</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> And having been warned by God in a dream not to return to Herod, the magi left for their own country by another way.
> </span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">Warned Not to Return to Herod</span>

After the magi had worshiped Jesus, God warned them in a dream not to go back to Herod.  

According to human reason they might have thought it was a wonderful opportunity if the great King Herod himself came to worship the Child. But God’s warning was clear.  

They obeyed and returned home by another way.  

This is another strong reminder not to lean on our own understanding or be impressed by great men, but to listen carefully to the leading of God.

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #F1C40F; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">🔑 Key Insight</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#5D4E00;">
> There is a strong desire in many Christians to get important people, rulers, or leaders to acknowledge Jesus.  
> But God often leads His people away from such human schemes.  
> The magi were wise enough to obey the dream rather than their own reason.
> </span>
> </div>

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 2:13–15</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> 13 Now when they had gone, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream and said, “Get up! Take the Child and His mother and flee to Egypt, and remain there until I tell you; for Herod is going to search for the Child to destroy Him.”<br>
> 14 So Joseph got up and took the Child and His mother while it was still night, and left for Egypt.<br>
> 15 He remained there until the death of Herod. This was to fulfill what had been spoken by the Lord through the prophet: “<span style="background-color:#FFE4E1; padding:1px 4px; border-radius:3px;">OUT OF EGYPT I CALLED MY SON</span>.”
> </span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">The Flight to Egypt</span>

This is the third dream in which God spoke to Joseph (the first was in Matthew 1, the second was the warning to the magi).  

Joseph did not argue or question. He did not say, “Lord, why don’t You just kill Herod?” He simply obeyed.  

He arose that same night, took the Child and His mother, and fled to Egypt.  

They remained there until Herod died. This fulfilled Hosea 11:1 — “Out of Egypt I called My Son.”  

God’s ways are higher than our ways. We may not always understand why He chooses a particular path, but we can trust Him.

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 2:16–18</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> 16 Then when Herod saw that he had been tricked by the magi, he became very enraged, and sent and slew all the male children who were in Bethlehem and all its vicinity, from two years old and under…<br>
> 17 Then what had been spoken through Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled:<br>
> 18 “A VOICE WAS HEARD IN RAMAH, WEEPING AND GREAT MOURNING, RACHEL WEEPING FOR HER CHILDREN; AND SHE REFUSED TO BE COMFORTED, BECAUSE THEY WERE NO MORE.”
> </span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">Herod’s Massacre</span>

When Herod realised the magi had not returned, he was furious. He ordered the killing of all the boys in Bethlehem two years old and under.  

He thought he had wiped out the threat to his throne. But God had already protected His Son.  

No man can successfully fight against God. Throughout history, many have tried to destroy a pure testimony for Christ — political powers, religious systems, and personal kingdoms — but they have never succeeded.

This tragic event also fulfilled Jeremiah 31:15.

> <span style="color:#0B3D1B;">
> God always protects His testimony.  
> Herod thought he had succeeded, but he failed completely.

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 2:19–22</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> 19 But when Herod died, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt, and said,<br>
> 20 “Get up, take the Child and His mother, and go into the land of Israel; for those who sought the Child’s life are dead.”<br>
> 21 So Joseph got up, took the Child and His mother, and came into the land of Israel.<br>
> 22 But when he heard that Archelaus was reigning over Judea in place of his father Herod, he was afraid to go there. Then after being warned by God in a dream, he left for the regions of Galilee…
> </span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">The Return from Egypt</span>

After Herod died, God spoke to Joseph again in a dream (his fourth dream).  

Joseph immediately obeyed and returned to the land of Israel.  

When he heard that Archelaus was ruling in Judea, he was afraid. God warned him once more, and he went to Galilee and settled in Nazareth.  

Joseph’s life is a beautiful example of the **obedience of faith**. He never argued with God. He simply did what the Lord told him, every single time.

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Main Lessons from This Passage</span>

1. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Do not lean on your own reason</span>  
   The magi and Joseph both had to set aside human reasoning and obey God’s clear direction.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 2:12-13 · Proverbs 3:5-6 · Isaiah 55:8-9</span>

2. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">God’s protection is perfect</span>  
   Herod’s plan failed completely because God had already moved His Son to safety.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 2:13-15 · Psalm 91:1-4 · Romans 8:31</span>

3. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Obedience of faith is immediate</span>  
   Joseph arose “while it was still night.” He did not delay or negotiate with God.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 2:14 · Hebrews 11:8 · Genesis 22:3</span>

4. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">God’s ways are higher than ours</span>  
   We may wonder why God did not simply remove Herod. His reasons are perfect even when we do not understand them.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Isaiah 55:8-9 · Romans 11:33 · Job 42:2-3</span>

5. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">God provides before the need arises</span>  
   The gifts of the magi (especially the gold) supplied what Joseph and Mary needed for the long stay in Egypt.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 2:11 · Philippians 4:19 · 2 Corinthians 9:8</span>
