### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 4:12–17</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> 12 Now when Jesus heard that John had been taken into custody, He withdrew into Galilee;<br>
> 13 and leaving Nazareth, He came and settled in Capernaum, which is by the sea, in the region of Zebulun and Naphtali.<br>
> 14 This was to fulfill what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet:<br>
> 15 “The land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali, by the way of the sea, beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles—<br>
> 16 The people who were sitting in darkness saw a great Light, and those who were sitting in the land and shadow of death, upon them a Light dawned.”<br>
> 17 From that time Jesus began to preach and say, “<span style="background-color:#FFE4E1; padding:1px 4px; border-radius:3px;">Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand</span>.”
> </span>

<img src="https://i.pinimg.com/1200x/6d/b0/0d/6db00d5b8cb5200192ef8b73c579b2c1.jpg" width="500" alt="Jesus begins His ministry">

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">From Nazareth to Capernaum</span>

After John was arrested, Jesus withdrew into Galilee and settled in Capernaum. This move was not random. It fulfilled Isaiah’s prophecy that a great light would dawn on the people sitting in darkness in Galilee of the Gentiles.

God has a specific plan for every life (Ephesians 2:10). Jesus moved according to the Father’s plan, not personal convenience. After overcoming temptation, He returned in the power of the Spirit. Temptation strengthens spiritual muscles so that we can handle the power of God faithfully.

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">The Message Continues</span>

From that time Jesus began to preach the same message John had preached: “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.” The gospel is a relay race through history. The message of repentance and the kingdom of heaven continues. The kingdom of heaven stands against the kingdom of self. True repentance means turning from living for self to living for God’s interests.

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #F1C40F; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">🔑 Key Insight</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#5D4E00;">
> • Jesus moved according to God’s predetermined plan, fulfilling Scripture.<br>
> • Temptation prepares us to handle spiritual power faithfully.<br>
> • The message of repentance and the kingdom of heaven continues from John to Jesus.<br>
> • True repentance is turning from the kingdom of self to the rule of God.
> </span>
> </div>

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 4:18–22</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> 18 Now as Jesus was walking by the Sea of Galilee, He saw two brothers, Simon who was called Peter, and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea; for they were fishermen.<br>
> 19 And He said to them, “<span style="background-color:#FFE4E1; padding:1px 4px; border-radius:3px;">Follow Me, and I will make you fishers of men</span>.”<br>
> 20 Immediately they left their nets and followed Him.<br>
> 21 Going on from there He saw two other brothers, James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, in the boat with Zebedee their father, mending their nets; and He called them.<br>
> 22 Immediately they left the boat and their father, and followed Him.
> </span>

<img src="https://achristianpilgrim.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/00000000000000000000000000-jesus-calling-disciples-clipart-5.jpg" width="500" alt="Jesus calls the first disciples">

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">The Call to Follow</span>

Jesus called men who were already employed and faithful in their earthly work. He did not call idle people. He expects us to be faithful in our secular responsibilities before calling us into full-time service.

The response was immediate. They left their nets, their boat, and their father. Discipleship requires breaking earthly attachments and putting Jesus first. Slow obedience is disobedience; we can miss God’s plan if we delay.

Jesus makes us “fishers of men.” The call is to follow Him, and He will do the transforming work in us.

> <div style="background-color:#D4EDDA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #28A745; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#155724; font-weight:bold;">✅ Important Truth</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#0B3D1B;">
> • Jesus calls those who are already faithful in their earthly responsibilities.<br>
> • True discipleship requires immediate obedience and leaving earthly attachments.<br>
> • He makes us fishers of men — the transformation is His work as we follow Him.<br>
> • Delayed obedience can cause us to miss God’s plan for our lives.
> </span>
> </div>

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 4:23–25</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> 23 Jesus was going throughout all Galilee, teaching in their synagogues and proclaiming the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every kind of disease and every kind of sickness among the people.<br>
> 24 The news about Him spread throughout all Syria; and they brought to Him all who were ill, those suffering with various diseases and pains, demoniacs, epileptics, paralytics; and He healed them.<br>
> 25 Large crowds followed Him from Galilee and the Decapolis and Jerusalem and Judea and from beyond the Jordan.
> </span>

<img src="https://media.bible.art/f16046d3-72ee-42c6-bf00-07b84272e7fa-compressed.jpg" width="500" alt="Jesus teaching and healing in Galilee">

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">Teaching, Proclaiming, and Healing</span>

Jesus’ ministry is summarised in three activities: teaching in the synagogues, proclaiming the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every kind of disease.

The gospel of the kingdom is not merely the forgiveness of sins (which was already known in the Old Testament). It is the good news that God can rule in our hearts, that we can deny self, and that our minds can be set on heavenly things.

Healing opened doors for the proclamation of the gospel. Crowds came from far and wide. The pattern remains: genuine power and compassion open the way for the message of the kingdom.

> <div style="background-color:#D4EDDA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #28A745; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#155724; font-weight:bold;">✅ Important Truth</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#0B3D1B;">
> • Jesus’ ministry combined teaching, proclaiming the kingdom, and healing.<br>
> • The gospel of the kingdom is the rule of God in the heart and the denial of self.<br>
> • Healing and compassion open doors for the message.<br>
> • Crowds followed because of the reality of God’s power and love.
> </span>
> </div>

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Main Lessons from This Passage</span>

1. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">God has a specific plan for every life</span>  
   Jesus moved according to prophecy and the Father’s will.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 4:13-16 · Ephesians 2:10 · Proverbs 3:5-6</span>

2. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">The message of repentance continues</span>  
   From John to Jesus the call remains: turn from self to the kingdom of heaven.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 4:17 · Matthew 3:2 · Acts 2:38</span>

3. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Immediate obedience is required</span>  
   The disciples left everything at once. Delayed response can mean missed opportunities.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 4:20, 22 · Luke 9:59-62 · Hebrews 3:15</span>

4. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Faithfulness in ordinary work precedes special calling</span>  
   Jesus called working men who were diligent in their daily tasks.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 4:18-22 · Colossians 3:23 · Luke 16:10</span>

5. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">The gospel of the kingdom includes power and compassion</span>  
   Teaching, proclaiming, and healing went together.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 4:23 · Matthew 9:35 · Acts 10:38</span>
