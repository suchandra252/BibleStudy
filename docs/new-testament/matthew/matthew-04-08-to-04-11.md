### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 4:8–9</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> 8 Again, the devil took Him to a very high mountain and showed Him all the kingdoms of the world and their glory;<br>
> 9 and he said to Him, “All these things I will give You, if You fall down and <span style="background-color:#FFE4E1; padding:1px 4px; border-radius:3px;">worship me</span>.”
> </span>

<img src="https://media.freebibleimages.org/stories/FB_GNPI_014_Jesus_Temptation/source-jpeg/06_FB_GNPI_014_Jesus_Temptation_1024.jpg?1725894120" width="500">
#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">The Third Temptation – The Kingdoms of the World</span>

The devil took Jesus to a very high mountain and showed Him all the kingdoms of the world and their glory. Then he offered them to Jesus on one condition: “Fall down and worship me.”

This temptation has two clear aspects. First, it is the temptation to gain the honour, power, and glory of the world through compromise. In everyday life this can mean telling a lie, giving a bribe, or remaining silent about certain truths in order to gain acceptance or a wider audience. In religious circles it can mean softening the message of the cross so that more people will follow us.

Second, it is the temptation to take a shortcut to accomplish God’s purpose — to gain the kingdoms without going the way of the cross. The Father’s plan was that Jesus would win the kingdoms through suffering and death. The devil offered an easier route that bypassed the cross completely.

The devil tempts in every kind of situation: in the wilderness of hardship, in the holy city of religious activity, and on the high mountain of possible achievement and glory.

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #F1C40F; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">🔑 Key Insight</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#5D4E00;">
> • The third temptation is to gain the glory of the world through compromise.<br>
> • It is also the temptation to take a shortcut that bypasses the cross.<br>
> • The devil offers the kingdoms without the suffering God has appointed.<br>
> • Compromise for popularity or personal gain is a form of bowing the knee to Satan.<br>
> • The devil tempts in the wilderness, in religious settings, and on the mountain of achievement.
> </span>
> </div>

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">📌 Matthew 4:10–11</span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">NASB Text</span>

> <span style="color:#922B21; font-size:1.05em;">
> 10 Then Jesus said to him, “Go, Satan! For it is written, ‘You shall <span style="background-color:#FFE4E1; padding:1px 4px; border-radius:3px;">worship the Lord your God, and serve Him only</span>.’”<br>
> 11 Then the devil left Him; and behold, angels came and began to minister to Him.
> </span>

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">Worship and Serve Him Only</span>

Jesus answered with the same weapon He used in the previous temptations: “It is written.” He quoted Deuteronomy 6:13: “You shall worship the Lord your God, and serve Him only.”

Notice the order: worship first, then service. True worship is the complete surrender of our bodies as a living sacrifice to God (Romans 12:1). Only from that place of total surrender can we serve God effectively. Service that does not flow out of worship easily becomes compromised and self-seeking.

Jesus did not enter into discussion with the devil. He simply stood on the written Word and commanded him to leave. Scripture is the final authority. The sword of the Spirit is the Word of God (Ephesians 6:17).

#### <span style="color:#6C3483;">The Devil Leaves and Angels Minister</span>

When Jesus stood firm, the devil left Him (though only for a season, as Luke records). God does not allow us to be tempted beyond what we are able to bear. He also provides strength and encouragement. Angels came and ministered to Jesus after the long period of testing.

This is the pattern of victory: refuse every compromise, refuse every shortcut that bypasses the cross, worship God alone, and stand on His Word. Then the enemy must leave, and God Himself will strengthen us.

> <div style="background-color:#D4EDDA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #28A745; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#155724; font-weight:bold;">✅ Important Truth</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#0B3D1B;">
> • Jesus overcame by the written Word alone — “It is written.”<br>
> • Worship comes before service; true worship is the surrender of our bodies to God.<br>
> • We must serve God only — never compromise for worldly glory or a wider audience.<br>
> • When we stand firm, the devil leaves and God sends strength and encouragement.<br>
> • There is no shortcut that bypasses the cross; God’s purpose is accomplished only through the way of the cross.
> </span>
> </div>

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Main Lessons from This Passage</span>

1. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Refuse every form of compromise</span>  
   Gaining the kingdoms of the world by bowing the knee in any area is a serious temptation.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 4:9 · Matthew 16:26 · 1 John 2:15-17</span>

2. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">There is no shortcut past the cross</span>  
   God’s purposes are fulfilled only through the way of self-denial and the cross.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 4:8-9 · Luke 9:23 · Hebrews 12:2</span>

3. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Worship first, then serve</span>  
   True service flows out of the complete surrender of our lives to God.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 4:10 · Romans 12:1 · Deuteronomy 6:13</span>

4. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">Overcome by the Word of God</span>  
   Jesus used no other weapon. Scripture is the sword of the Spirit and the final authority.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 4:10 · Ephesians 6:17 · Hebrews 4:12</span>

5. <span style="color:#6C3483; font-weight:bold;">God strengthens those who remain faithful</span>  
   When the devil leaves, God sends His help and encouragement.  
   <span style="color:#1A5276; font-size:0.95em;">→ Matthew 4:11 · 1 Corinthians 10:13 · Hebrews 1:14</span>
