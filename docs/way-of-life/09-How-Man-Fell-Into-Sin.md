### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Introduction</span>

This message looks at **Genesis 3** — how the first man and woman actually fell into sin.

If we understand how they fell, we can recognise the same methods the devil still uses today.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ The Devil Used Cleverness</span>

The serpent was more crafty than any other beast. Satan chose that creature because he usually works through **human cleverness**.

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #FFC107; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">💬 Key Quote</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#533F03;">
> The devil usually picks on clever people and uses human cleverness to deceive. When a preacher tries to impress his congregation with his cleverness, he is opening himself up to the devil. We must not trust our reason. We must trust our heavenly Father.
> </span>
> </div>

Even the Apostle Paul could be in danger when he trusted his own cleverness. The people who crucified Jesus were also very clever religious men.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Eve Changed God’s Word</span>

The devil first made Eve **question** God’s Word: “Has God really said…?”

Eve’s answer already contained two errors:

1. **She subtracted from God’s Word**  
   God had said, “You will **surely** die.”  
   Eve said something weaker — as if death was only a possibility.

2. **She added to God’s Word**  
   God had only said, “Do not eat.”  
   Eve added, “Do not even touch.”

> <div style="background-color:#F8D7DA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #DC3545; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#721C24; font-weight:bold;">⚠️ Serious Warning</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#491217;">
> Reducing the strength of God’s Word is common in Christendom today. Many preachers soften “you will surely die” into “God is loving, He will not punish.” Adding extra rules beyond Scripture leads to legalism and bondage — the same spirit the Pharisees had when they multiplied Sabbath laws.
> </span>
> </div>

God’s Word must be left **exactly as it is**. Subtracting from it weakens the warning. Adding to it produces bondage.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ The First Lie in the Bible</span>

God said:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“In the day that you eat from it you will surely die.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Genesis 2:17</span>

The devil said:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“You will surely not die.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Genesis 3:4</span>

This is the first lie in Scripture:  
**“Even if you disobey God, you will not be punished.”**

The devil still repeats the same lie:  
“God is a good God, a loving God — if you sin, He will not punish you. You will not die.”

> <span style="color:#276749;">“…God will send upon them a deluding influence so that they will believe what is false.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— 2 Thessalonians 2:11</span>

False prophets say “Peace, peace” when there is no peace. Many Christians are deceived into thinking sin is not serious and God will simply overlook it.

But Scripture is clear even for believers:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“If you are living according to the flesh, you must die.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Romans 8:13</span>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ The Devil Also Questioned God’s Love</span>

The serpent implied:  
“God is withholding this fruit because He does not really love you. He does not want you to become like Him.”

That is still the devil’s method: first question the Word, then question the Father’s love.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Satan Often Speaks Through Someone Close</span>

Satan tempted Eve directly. Then he reached **Adam through Eve**.

The same pattern is seen with Jesus:
- Satan tempted Jesus directly to avoid the cross.
- Later he spoke **through Peter**: “God forbid, Lord! This shall never happen to You.”

> <div style="background-color:#D1ECF1; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #17A2B8; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#0C5460; font-weight:bold;">💡 How to Recognise Satan’s Voice</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#062C33;">
> Satan’s voice always tells us to avoid the way of the cross and to seek our own. Even a close relative or a well-meaning friend can carry that voice without knowing it.
> </span>
> </div>

Adam ate the fruit to please Eve, without realising that Satan was speaking through her. Eve was deceived. Adam sinned with his eyes open.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ What Happened Immediately After They Sinned</span>

Their eyes were opened. They knew they were naked. They sewed fig leaves and hid — from each other and from God.

Earlier it was written:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“The man and his wife were both naked and were not ashamed.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Genesis 2:25</span>

After sin, shame entered. Fellowship was broken.

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #FFC107; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">💬 Deep Insight</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#533F03;">
> Sin makes a person very foolish. How can anyone hide from God behind a tree? Instead of running to God — the only true Friend who can help when we fall — they ran away from Him.
> </span>
> </div>

Fig leaves picture **human righteousness**:
- trying to cover failure by becoming more zealous
- giving more money
- looking more religious

That is what the Pharisees did. It cannot cover sin before God.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ God Came Searching</span>

God came walking in the garden and asked:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“Where are you?”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Genesis 3:9</span>

This was not because God did not know. It was the call of a Father seeking His fallen children.

Adam admitted that he heard God’s voice and hid because he was afraid and naked. God asked whether he had eaten from the forbidden tree.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ The Habit of Blaming Others</span>

Adam should have confessed and asked forgiveness. Instead he said:

“The woman **whom You gave me**…”

He blamed Eve — and he blamed God.

Eve then blamed the serpent.

> <div style="background-color:#F8D7DA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #DC3545; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#721C24; font-weight:bold;">⚠️ Lesson for Us</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#491217;">
> When sin comes in, we immediately point the finger at other people. That habit breaks fellowship with God. We must judge ourselves and be merciful toward the failures of others.
> </span>
> </div>

God will one day expose hidden things and motives.

> <span style="color:#276749;">“Every careless word that people speak, they shall give an accounting for it in the day of judgment.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Matthew 12:36–37</span>

> <span style="color:#276749;">“…the Lord… will both bring to light the things hidden in the darkness and disclose the motives of men’s hearts.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— 1 Corinthians 4:5</span>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Key Takeaways</span>

> <div style="background-color:#D4EDDA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #28A745; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#155724; font-weight:bold;">✅ Summary of Main Truths</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#0B3D1B;">
> • Satan works through cleverness; preachers must not rely on cleverness.<br>
> • Eve first subtracted from God’s Word, then added to it. Both are dangerous.<br>
> • Softening God’s warnings produces deception. Adding extra rules produces legalism.<br>
> • The first lie in the Bible is: “If you disobey, you will not die.” That lie is still preached today.<br>
> • The devil questions both God’s Word and God’s love.<br>
> • Satan often speaks through someone close to us, telling us to avoid the cross and seek our own.<br>
> • Eve was deceived; Adam sinned knowingly to please her.<br>
> • After sin, shame came, fellowship broke, and they hid from God and from each other.<br>
> • Fig leaves picture human efforts to cover sin. They cannot hide us from God.<br>
> • God still comes seeking: “Where are you?”<br>
> • Blaming others (or blaming God) keeps us from true repentance.<br>
> • Judge yourself. Be merciful to others.
> </span>
> </div>

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">🙏 Prayer</span>

Heavenly Father,  

Thank You for showing me how the first man and woman fell, so that I can recognise the same voice today.

Forgive me for every time I have softened Your Word, added my own rules, or believed the lie that sin will not bring death.

Deliver me from trusting cleverness instead of trusting You. Help me to recognise when Satan speaks through even a close person, calling me to avoid the cross and seek my own.

When I fall, do not let me hide behind fig leaves or blame others. Teach me to run to You, confess honestly, and judge myself.

Lord, please help me to get rid of this habit of blaming other people for my sin. Help me to judge myself.

In Jesus’ name, Amen.
