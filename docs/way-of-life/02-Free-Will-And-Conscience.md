### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Introduction – Recap</span>

Last time we saw the origin of evil.  

God created everything perfect in the beginning. The head of the angels (Lucifer) rebelled against God in **pride** and discontent with what God had given him. That is how the devil came into existence, and millions of angels who joined him also fell into sin.

Now an important question arises:  
**Why did God not stop this? Why didn’t He destroy them immediately? Why did God allow such evil to come forth in a perfect universe?**

To answer this, we must understand the function of **free will**.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Creation Without Free Will</span>

There are many things God created that do **not** have free will.

**Example: Stars and Planets**  
- God placed them in a particular orbit thousands of years ago.  
- They have to move in that orbit.  
- They can never change it.  
- They have no choice.  

Because of this, we have a perfect universe. We can even set our watches by the movement of the planets. There is perfect order and beauty wherever anything obeys God’s will perfectly.

If even one planet had free will, it could one day decide to move out of its orbit and create chaos.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Machines vs Children</span>

God could have made angels and man like machines or robots — programmed to obey Him perfectly. Then there would have been no disobedience in the universe at all.

But consider this:

Would you prefer a house full of computer-controlled robots, or one or two real children (even if they are sometimes disobedient)?

Most parents would rather have even one or two children than a hundred perfectly obedient robots.

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #FFC107; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">💬 Powerful Illustration</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#533F03;">
> On your birthday, your three-year-old child picks up a card and scribbles a birthday greeting for you. That scribbled card makes you weep with joy and want to hug your child. A computer-programmed greeting on the screen may look a hundred times better, but it does not move your heart the same way. Why? Because the child voluntarily chose to do it. The computer was only programmed.
> </span>
> </div>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Why God Gave Free Will</span>

God did not want machines. He wanted **children**.

When you have children, you take the risk that they may disobey you. A machine or scooter may break down, but it will never deliberately disobey you. Children can disobey — yet we still love them more than our machines.

God was willing to take that risk.

> <div style="background-color:#D1ECF1; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #17A2B8; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#0C5460; font-weight:bold;">💡 Deep Insight</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#062C33;">
> Even if God knew that out of 100 human beings, 99 would disobey Him and only one would obey, He still considered it worth it. One child who voluntarily loves and obeys Him brings more joy to God’s heart than a thousand perfect machines.
> </span>
> </div>

The entire universe is enormous, and the earth is like a tiny speck of sand. Yet God’s interest is not primarily in the millions of stars. His interest is in small people like you and me — longing to see us loving Him and obeying Him from the heart.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Free Will and the Possibility of Disobedience</span>

When God gives free will, there is always the possibility of disobedience. That is the only way to have real children instead of machines.

Lucifer had free will, and that is why he was able to rebel.

God knew the risk, yet He still chose to give free will because He wanted a family of sons and daughters who would love Him voluntarily.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ The Second Essential Gift – Conscience</span>

Free will alone is not enough.  

God also gave man something that even animals do not have — **conscience**.

- Planets, trees, mountains and machines have no conscience.  
- Animals have a measure of free will (a dog may or may not obey), but they have **no conscience**.  
- No animal feels a sense of guilt after doing something wrong.

Conscience is the inner voice that tells a human being, “You did something wrong.”

This is the second essential gift God gave so that man could become His child.

> <span style="color:#0B3D1B;">
> • God allowed free will because He wanted children, not machines.<br>
> • Perfect obedience without choice (like planets or robots) cannot bring God the joy of voluntary love.<br>
> • Free will always carries the risk of disobedience, but God considered the risk worth taking.<br>
> • Even if only a few choose to love and obey Him, it brings Him greater joy than a universe of programmed beings.<br>
> • Conscience is the second vital gift that distinguishes man from animals and enables true moral responsibility.<br>
> • God longs for people who will freely choose to love and obey Him from the heart.
