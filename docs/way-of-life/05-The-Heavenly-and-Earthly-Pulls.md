<h2 style="color:#553c9a;">Introduction</h2>

The most important thing for every human being is to **know God** and receive His life.  

<span style="color:#9b2c2c;"><mark>If we do not know God and do not receive His life, we will gradually descend to the level of animals.</mark></span>

---

<h2 style="color:#553c9a;">Why God Created Animals and Man on the Same Day</h2>

In Genesis 1, God created the animals and man on the **same day** — the sixth day.  

This was deliberate.  

- He could have created animals on the fifth day with the fish.  
- Instead, He created them on the same day as man to show that **physically** man and animals are very similar.  

Both have:
- Flesh, blood, bones  
- Heart, liver, and internal organs  
- Eyes, ears, and the ability to feel pain  
- Both die and return to dust  

God formed man from the dust of the ground (Genesis 2). Animals were created by His spoken word.  

But then something completely different happened.

---

<h2 style="color:#553c9a;">God Breathed His Spirit into Man</h2>

> <span style="color:#276749;">“The Lord God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Genesis 2:7</span>

God did **not** do this for any animal.  

He breathed His own Spirit into man.  

> <span style="color:#276749;">“God created man in His own image… male and female He created them.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Genesis 1:27</span>

God is Spirit. Therefore the image of God in man is the **spirit** that God placed within him — not the physical body.  

Animals have minds (the serpent in Genesis 3 was more intelligent than other animals), but they have **no spirit** and **no conscience**.

<span style="color:#9b2c2c;"><u>Man is the only created being on earth that has a spirit.</u></span>

---

<h2 style="color:#553c9a;">Living Like Animals</h2>

If a man lives only for the desires of his body — **food, sex, and sleep** — he is living exactly like an animal.  

- Animals always look **down** for food.  
- Many people also look down — for money, power, pleasure, and sex.  
- Animals fight and kill without guilt.  
- Animals have no faithfulness in mating.  

When human beings live the same way (fighting, unfaithfulness, lust, violence), they are living at the **animal level**.

---

<h2 style="color:#553c9a;">The Two Pulls in Every Human Life</h2>

Every human being stands between **two powerful pulls**:

### 1. The Earthly Pull (Downward)
- Comes from the body and the flesh  
- Pulls towards selfishness, lust, greed, anger, and worldly pleasure  
- Makes a person more and more like an animal  

### 2. The Heavenly Pull (Upward)
- Comes from the spirit that God breathed into man  
- Speaks first through the **conscience**  
- Later, for believers, through the **Holy Spirit**  
- Pulls a person upward towards God, purity, and eternal life  

> <span style="color:#c05621;">“We are between these two pulls. One pulling us down. The other pulling us up. An animal has nothing pulling it up. But we do — all human beings have it, including those who call themselves atheists.”</span>  
> — Zac Poonen

Even atheists, when they are dying or drowning, suddenly cry out to God. That is the upward pull still working in them.

God designed man so that the **upward heavenly pull** should control every area of his life — not the downward earthly pull.

---

<h2 style="color:#553c9a;">The Role of Conscience</h2>

Conscience is the first expression of the heavenly pull.  

- Animals have **no conscience**. They never feel guilty.  
- When a man ignores his conscience, he is descending to the animal level.  

Conscience tells every human being that he is answerable to a higher power. That is why even uneducated people in jungles worship something (sun, tree, stone, etc.). Animals never worship.

However, conscience is **not a perfect guide**.  
- It can become sensitive or insensitive depending on upbringing and culture.  
- In a godly home, conscience remains tender.  
- In a godless home, it quickly becomes hard.

---

<h2 style="color:#553c9a;">The Holy Spirit — Higher than Conscience and the Written Word</h2>

After a person receives Christ and opens his life to the Holy Spirit, God begins to speak through the **Holy Spirit**.  

This is higher than conscience.  

<span style="color:#9b2c2c;"><mark>The Word of God gives us principles, but it will not tell you everything specifically.</mark></span>

- The Bible will not tell you **which movie** to watch.  
- The Bible will not tell you **which job** to take.  
- The Bible will not tell you **what programme** to watch on television.  
- The Bible will not tell you every daily decision you must make.  

But the **Holy Spirit** will.  

He will warn you.  
He will check you.  
He will give you a clear sense of what is good for your spirit and what is harmful.

This privilege of being led by the Holy Spirit was not available in the Old Testament. It began only on the Day of Pentecost (Acts 2), when the Holy Spirit came to dwell inside believers.

God originally wanted Adam to live by this life (symbolised by the tree of life). Adam refused. Now, through the Holy Spirit, that same life is available to us.

---

<h2 style="color:#553c9a;">Daily Decisions Reveal Which Pull We Follow</h2>

Every day we make many decisions without much thought:

- What book to read  
- What to watch  
- What to buy  
- How to spend time  
- How to react  

Each decision is a response to either the **earthly pull** or the **heavenly pull**.

**Example given by Zac Poonen:**  
When deciding whether to watch a certain movie —  
- The downward pull says: “It is sexy… it is entertaining… it is good.”  
- The upward pull says: “This will harm your spirit.”  

You must choose.  

<span style="color:#9b2c2c;"><u>If you keep choosing the downward pull year after year, you will become more and more like an animal.</u></span>  

<span style="color:#9b2c2c;"><u>If you keep choosing the upward pull, you will become more and more godly.</u></span>

In ten years’ time, two people who started at the same place can become completely different — one godly and one carnal — simply because of the daily decisions they made.

Ultimately, whether a person goes to hell or lives with God for eternity is the result of his own decisions.

---

<h2 style="color:#553c9a;">Key Takeaways / Important Truths</h2>

- Man and animals were created on the same day to show physical similarity.  
- God breathed His Spirit only into man — this is what makes man different.  
- Living only for body desires (food, sex, sleep, money, power) is living at the animal level.  
- Every human being has two pulls: earthly (downward) and heavenly (upward).  
- Conscience is the first voice of the heavenly pull.  
- Ignoring conscience reduces a person to the level of an animal.  
- The Holy Spirit is a higher and clearer guide than conscience.  
- <mark>The written Word of God gives principles, but the Holy Spirit guides us in specific daily decisions (movies, jobs, entertainment, etc.).</mark>  
- Every daily decision is a response to one of the two pulls.  
- Consistent response to the heavenly pull produces a godly life.  
- Consistent response to the earthly pull produces an animal-like life.  
- Our eternal destiny is shaped by the decisions we make while on earth.
