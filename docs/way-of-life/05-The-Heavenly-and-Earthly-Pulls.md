<h1 style="color:#1a365d;">The Heavenly and Earthly Pulls</h1>
**Zac Poonen**  
*Pathway to Perfection – Message 05*  
(With Tamil Translation)

---

<h2 style="color:#553c9a;">Introduction</h2>

God created animals and man on the **same day** — the sixth day. This was deliberate. Physically, man and animals are very similar. Both have flesh, blood, bones, eyes, ears, and feel pain. Both die.

But there is a **vast difference**.

God formed man from the dust of the ground and **breathed into his nostrils the breath of life**, and man became a living soul.

> <span style="color:#c05621;">“The Lord God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Genesis 2:7</span>

Man alone was made in the **image of God**.

> <span style="color:#276749;">“God created man in His own image… male and female He created them.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Genesis 1:27</span>

God is Spirit. Therefore, the image of God in man is the **spirit** that God placed within him.

---

<h2 style="color:#553c9a;">Animals Have No Spirit</h2>

Animals have a body and a mind. They can feel, remember, and react. But they have **no spirit** and **no conscience**.

- Animals live only for the desires of the body: food, sex, and sleep.
- They always look **down** — for grass, for prey, for mates.
- They fight, kill, and take what they want without any sense of guilt.
- There is no faithfulness or higher purpose in them.

<span style="color:#9b2c2c;"><mark>If a human being lives only for the desires of the body — food, sex, money, comfort, and sleep — he is living exactly like an animal.</mark></span>

---

<h2 style="color:#553c9a;">Two Pulls in Every Human Life</h2>

Every human being stands between **two powerful pulls**:

1. **The Earthly Pull** (Downward)  
   - Towards the body and its desires  
   - Towards selfishness, lust, anger, greed, and violence  
   - This pull makes a man more and more like an animal

2. **The Heavenly Pull** (Upward)  
   - Through the **conscience** that God has placed in every person  
   - And, for believers, through the **Holy Spirit**  
   - This pull draws a man towards God, purity, and eternal life

<span style="color:#9b2c2c;"><u>We are constantly being pulled in these two directions.</u></span>

---

<h2 style="color:#553c9a;">The Role of Conscience</h2>

Even people who have never heard the gospel have a conscience. That is why uneducated tribes still worship something greater than themselves. Conscience tells man that he is accountable to a higher power.

- When you **listen** to your conscience, you move upward.
- When you **ignore** your conscience, you descend to the level of an animal.

Conscience is not perfect. It can be shaped by culture, upbringing, or even hardened by repeated sin. That is why God has given us something better:

- The **Word of God**
- The **Holy Spirit**

The Holy Spirit is the higher and clearer voice for those who belong to Christ.

---

<h2 style="color:#553c9a;">Daily Decisions Determine Destiny</h2>

Every day we make many small decisions:

- What to watch
- How to spend money
- How to speak
- How to react when angry
- Whether to seek God or seek pleasure

Each decision is a response to either the **earthly pull** or the **heavenly pull**.

> <span style="color:#c05621;">“We are between these two pulls. One pulling us down. The other pulling us up… Ultimately, when people go to hell for eternity, it is their own decision.”</span>  
> — Zac Poonen

<span style="color:#9b2c2c;"><mark>Over time, the direction we consistently choose shapes our character and our eternal destiny.</mark></span>

---

<h2 style="color:#553c9a;">God’s Original Plan</h2>

God never intended man to live by the body alone. He offered Adam the tree of life — a picture of living by the life of the Holy Spirit.

After Pentecost, every believer has access to that same Spirit. The Holy Spirit within us is the strong upward pull that can overcome the downward pull of the flesh.

---

<h2 style="color:#553c9a;">Key Takeaways / Important Truths</h2>

- Man is unique because God breathed a **spirit** into him.
- Animals live only for bodily desires; man is called to live for God.
- There are **two pulls** in every life — earthly (downward) and heavenly (upward).
- Ignoring conscience reduces a person to the level of an animal.
- The Holy Spirit is the strongest heavenly pull available to believers.
- <u>Every daily decision is a response to one of these two pulls.</u>
- Consistent response to the heavenly pull leads to godliness and eternal life.
- Consistent response to the earthly pull leads to spiritual death and eternal separation from God.
- Our final destiny is the result of the choices we make while on earth.

---

<h2 style="color:#2c7a7b;">Prayer</h2>

Heavenly Father,
