### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Welcome – Words of Life</span>

Welcome once again to **Words of Life**.

The world is full of words. Politicians speak words. Newspapers speak words. But here we want the words that give life.

Today we consider **sin**, because if we do not see how serious sin is, we will never appreciate what Christ did on the cross.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Why Did Christ Have to Die?</span>

Why did Christ have to die?  

That question itself shows us how serious sin is in God’s eyes.

> <div style="background-color:#F8D7DA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #DC3545; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#721C24; font-weight:bold;">⚠️ Serious Warning</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#491217;">
> If you want to know how serious sin is, remember this: to forgive your sin, God had to send His own Son to die on the cross. God could not find another way to forgive our sin except by sending His own Son to suffer and die.
> </span>
> </div>

The suffering of Jesus was not only physical agony. The greatest suffering was **separation from God**. We can never fully understand what a tremendous suffering that was.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ If There Were Other Ways, Why the Cross?</span>

Many other religions claim to provide another way of salvation.

Then a simple question must be asked:

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #FFC107; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">💬 Key Question from Zac Poonen</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#533F03;">
> Why in the world would God send His Son to suffer so much and die, if there are other ways of salvation? Why should a father send his son to die if there is a much easier way to save that person?
> </span>
> </div>

The very fact of the cross proves that there was **no other way**.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ “You Are Narrow-Minded”</span>

Sometimes people accuse Christians of being narrow-minded because Jesus said:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“I am the way… no one comes to the Father but through Me.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— John 14:6</span>

But consider this honestly:

- You and I do **not** know the way to heaven. We have never been there.
- There is only **one Person** who came from heaven who can tell us how to get there.
- That Person is Jesus Christ.

So this is not pride. It is simply accepting the testimony of the only One who came from there.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ God Never Forces Anyone</span>

Throughout the Bible, God wants us to decide with our own **free will**.

Jesus says, in effect:  
“If you open the door, I will come in. If you do not open, I will not come in.”

> <div style="background-color:#D1ECF1; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #17A2B8; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#0C5460; font-weight:bold;">💡 Core Truth</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#062C33;">
> We do not believe in forcible conversions. God never pushes His way into a person’s life. He knocks. He waits to be invited.
> </span>
> </div>

If people do not agree with us, we do not fight with them.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Truth Is Still Truth Even If Others Disagree</span>

Zac Poonen gives a personal example:

He has met people who still believe that the **sun goes around the earth**. He tells them that is not true. If they disagree, he will agree to disagree in a decent, gentle manner. He can love them — but he cannot accept that as the truth.

For thousands of years many people believed something that was not actual fact.  

In the same way, many people may reject Jesus as the only way. That does not change the fact:

**Jesus Christ is the only way to heaven.**

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ The Root of Sin – Lack of Trust in God</span>

The seriousness of sin is seen in the cross.  

But sin is more than wrong actions. At its root, sin is a **lack of trust in God**.

In Genesis, at the beginning of sin in the human race, the first man and the first woman stopped trusting God. The devil spoke cleverly. Eve listened to the devil’s reasons and was deceived.

Disobedience to God is based on **lack of faith in God** — that means **not believing in God’s perfect love for us**.

> <div style="background-color:#F8D7DA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #DC3545; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#721C24; font-weight:bold;">⚠️ False Picture of God</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#491217;">
> Unbelief takes a picture of God as a very selfish Person who does not want you to have anything good in your life. That is the lie the devil first told Eve — and he still tells it today.
> </span>
> </div>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Obedience Must Be Based on Trust, Not Fear</span>

Obedience to God must be based on **trust**.  

It is not enough to say, “I obey Him just because I have to obey Him.”

**Obedience based on fear** was accepted in the Old Testament, when people did not have much light. God accepted that type of obedience — just as a father would accept obedience from his three-year-old son who obeys mainly out of fear.

But that is not God’s final desire.

God wants our obedience to come **out of trust**.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ The Child Who Does Not Want to Go to School</span>

Think of a small child who does not want to go to school.

- If the father has to spend one hour every morning explaining all the advantages of school, nothing will get done.
- The child cannot be given ice cream for breakfast, lunch and dinner just because he likes it.
- The child must eat rice and vegetables even before he understands why.
- If you try to explain the reason for everything to little children, you will never get anything done in the house.

Later, when they grow up, they understand that the parent was acting for their good.

In the same way, God does not always explain every command in advance. He asks us to trust that He is a good Father.

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #FFC107; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">💬 Important Statement</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#533F03;">
> If you want to understand the reason for everything before you obey, you may not believe the Bible. You will not be able to know the Bible unless you know Jesus Christ.
> </span>
> </div>

Knowing Jesus first shows us what the Father is really like. Then the commands of Scripture begin to make sense as the words of a loving Father.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ High Standards Are Not a Reason to Stay Away</span>

Sometimes people say,  
“We don’t want to join this church because the standards are so high.”

That reaction often comes from the same false picture of God — as if God is a hard master who only wants to restrict us.  

In reality, God’s high standard is the expression of His love. He wants to save us **from** sin, not leave us comfortable in it.
