### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Introduction</span>

The world is full of words — from politicians, newspapers, and many voices. This series is called **Words of Life** because these are the words that lead us to God’s eternal life.

In this message we look at **sin and salvation** — how serious sin is in God’s eyes, and how God has provided the only way of salvation.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ How Serious Is Sin?</span>

If you want to know how serious sin is in God’s eyes, remember this:

> <div style="background-color:#F8D7DA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #DC3545; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#721C24; font-weight:bold;">⚠️ Serious Warning</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#491217;">
> To forgive your sin, God had to send His own Son to die on the cross. There was no other way. If there were other ways of salvation, why would God send His Son to suffer such agony and even experience separation from the Father?
> </span>
> </div>

The cross is the clearest proof that sin is not a small matter. It is so serious that only the death of the sinless Son of God could pay for it.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Jesus Is the Only Way</span>

> <span style="color:#276749;">“I am the way, and the truth, and the life; no one comes to the Father but through Me.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— John 14:6</span>

Some people say this is narrow-minded. But consider: only Jesus came from heaven. Only He knows the way back to the Father. No other religious teacher came from heaven or rose from the dead.

Jesus never forces anyone. He knocks at the door of the heart. He enters only if He is invited. Salvation is a free-will decision, not a forced conversion.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ The Root of Sin – Lack of Trust</span>

Sin began in the human race when the first man and woman listened to the devil instead of trusting God.

The devil painted a false picture of God — as if God were selfish and was keeping something good from them. Once they doubted God’s perfect love, disobedience followed.

> <div style="background-color:#D1ECF1; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #17A2B8; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#0C5460; font-weight:bold;">💡 Deep Insight</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#062C33;">
> Disobedience to God is based on lack of faith in God. It means not believing in God’s perfect love for us. When we doubt His goodness, we create a false image of God in our minds, and then we cannot understand the Bible rightly.
> </span>
> </div>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Fear vs Trust in Obedience</span>

In the Old Testament, God accepted obedience that came from fear — like a father accepting obedience from a three-year-old child who obeys mainly because he is afraid.

But that is not God’s final desire.

God wants obedience that comes from **trust** — like a grown child who obeys because he is convinced that his father loves him and knows what is best.

A small child may refuse to go to school because he does not yet trust the parent’s wisdom. The parent may still insist, but the goal is that the child will one day obey out of understanding and love, not only fear.

In the same way, God wants us to obey Him because we believe He is perfectly good and perfectly loving.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Faith Leads to Obedience</span>

True faith in God’s love produces willing obedience.

If we do not know Jesus, we will not know the Father rightly. Jesus came to show us what God is really like — full of grace and truth.

Without knowing Christ, people easily think of God as harsh, selfish, or distant. That wrong picture of God is itself a fruit of unbelief, and it keeps people in sin.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Key Takeaways</span>

> • The root of sin is unbelief — not trusting God’s perfect love.<br>
> • Old Testament obedience often came from fear; New Covenant obedience should come from trust.<br>
> • A wrong picture of God as selfish or harsh comes from not knowing Jesus.<br>
> • Faith in God’s goodness leads to willing obedience and true salvation.

> </div>
