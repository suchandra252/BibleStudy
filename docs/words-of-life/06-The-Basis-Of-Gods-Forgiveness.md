### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Introduction – Recap</span>

In previous messages we saw that human beings face two pulls: a downward pull towards the body (food, sex, sleep, earthly things) and an upward pull from God. Animals only have the downward pull.  

When a person lives only by the downward pull, he becomes like an animal. True godliness is responding to the needs of the body according to God’s principles — without harming others or one’s own body.

Sin is violating the divine principles that God has placed in our conscience. Every human being has committed countless sins and stands guilty before God, because we are responsible beings with a conscience (unlike animals).

Even evil thoughts make us guilty:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“Everyone who looks at a woman with lust for her has already committed adultery with her in his heart.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Matthew 5:28</span>

> <span style="color:#276749;">“Everyone who is angry with his brother shall be guilty… and whoever says, ‘You fool,’ shall be guilty enough to go into the fiery hell.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Matthew 5:22</span>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Common Misconceptions About Forgiveness</span>

Many people have wrong ideas about how God forgives:

1. **“God is good, so He will overlook my sins.”**  
2. **“If I say I am sorry, God will forgive me.”**  
3. **“At the end of life, God will weigh my good deeds against my bad deeds. If the good is more, I will go to heaven.”**

> <div style="background-color:#F8D7DA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #DC3545; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#721C24; font-weight:bold;">⚠️ Serious Warning</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#491217;">
> All three of these ideas are completely wrong. God cannot forgive simply because He is loving, or because we feel sorry, or because we did some good deeds. Even our “good deeds” can be evil in God’s eyes if they are done with selfish motives — for money, honour, praise, or personal profit.
> </span>
> </div>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Even Our Righteous Deeds Are Filthy</span>

> <span style="color:#276749;">“All our righteous deeds are like a filthy garment.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Isaiah 64:6</span>

A person with a diseased hand may offer a good apple. The apple itself is good, but because the hand is diseased, the offering is unacceptable.  

In the same way, God looks at the **heart and motive**, not just the outward action.  

Preaching for money, praying to be seen by others, or doing charity for reputation are all evil in God’s eyes — even though they look good outwardly (see Jesus driving out the temple sellers in John 2).

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ God Is Both Loving and Infinitely Just</span>

God is perfect love, but He is also infinitely just.  

If God simply overlooked sin because of love, the entire moral order of the universe would collapse. Justice must be satisfied.

> <div style="background-color:#D1ECF1; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #17A2B8; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#0C5460; font-weight:bold;">💡 Deep Insight</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#062C33;">
> The punishment for our sin is eternal separation from God. God cannot simply cancel that punishment without a just basis. Love alone cannot forgive. Justice must be fully satisfied first.
> </span>
> </div>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ The Beautiful Solution – The Cross</span>

God’s solution is perfect.  

He sent His Son (the second Person of the Trinity) into the world in human form. Jesus lived a perfect life and then died on the cross, taking the full punishment for our sins — eternal separation from God.

The Father did not die. The Son died and experienced that terrible separation (“My God, My God, why have You forsaken Me?”).

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #FFC107; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">💬 Powerful Analogy</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#533F03;">
> Imagine a judge whose own son is brought before him as a criminal. The judge must pass the correct sentence according to the law — a fine of two million rupees. After pronouncing the judgment, the judge steps down from the bench, removes his judicial robes, and writes a personal cheque from his own account to pay the full fine.  
> <br>
> Justice is completely satisfied (the son was sentenced as a criminal). Love is also fully expressed (the father himself paid the debt).  
> <br>
> This is exactly what God did for us through Jesus Christ.
> </span>
> </div>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ How We Receive Forgiveness</span>

Forgiveness cannot be earned by works, sorrow, or good deeds.  

It is received simply by **faith** — by believing that Jesus has already paid the full price for our sins, and by thanking Him and receiving Him as Saviour.

> <div style="background-color:#D4EDDA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #28A745; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#155724; font-weight:bold;">✅ Simple Gospel Truth</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#0B3D1B;">
> You say: “Thank You, Lord Jesus, for dying for my sins. I receive You as my Saviour.”  
> It is that simple. Your sins can be completely forgiven.
> </span>
> </div>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Key Takeaways</span>

> <div style="background-color:#D4EDDA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #28A745; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#155724; font-weight:bold;">✅ Summary of Main Truths</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#0B3D1B;">
> • Every human being is guilty before God because of countless sins of thought, word, and deed.<br>
> • God cannot forgive simply because He is loving, or because we feel sorry, or because we did good deeds.<br>
> • Even our righteous deeds are like filthy garments when done with wrong motives.<br>
> • God is both perfect love and infinite justice. Justice must be satisfied.<br>
> • Jesus, the Son of God, paid the full punishment for our sins on the cross.<br>
> • Forgiveness is received freely by faith — by thanking Jesus and receiving Him as Saviour.<br>
> • We cannot earn forgiveness; we can only receive what Jesus has already paid for.
> </span>
> </div>
