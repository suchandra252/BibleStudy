### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Introduction – Recap</span>

In the last two sessions we saw how evil originated in this universe.  

God is a God full of love and goodness. Yet evil came because God gave the angels free will and a conscience. Some of them (led by Lucifer) chose to disobey God through **pride**, **discontentment**, and **rebellion** against His authority.

These three spirits are still active in the world today:
- Pride  
- Discontentment / grumbling / complaining  
- Rebellion against authority  

We see them in children rebelling against parents, people rebelling against authorities at work, and students rebelling against teachers.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ The Root vs the Fruit of Evil</span>

Most people think of evil only as murder, adultery, theft, etc.  

Those are only the **fruit** of evil.  

The **root** is:
- Pride  
- Discontentment  
- Rebellion against authority  
- Wanting to do my own thing / wanting my own way  

> <div style="background-color:#F8D7DA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #DC3545; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#721C24; font-weight:bold;">⚠️ Important Clarification</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#491217;">
> The world does not call pride, discontentment, or wanting one’s own way as “sin”. Even in many Christian churches these root issues are rarely preached. The devil has cleverly concealed the root of sin and only allowed people to see and deal with the outer manifestations (the fruit).
> </span>
> </div>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Dealing with the Root, Not Just the Fruit</span>

Think of a bad tree that keeps producing bad fruit.  

If you only keep cutting off the bad fruit with scissors, you will spend your whole life doing that. New fruit will keep coming.  

The permanent solution is to deal with the **root** — to pull the tree out from its roots.

In the same way, we all have an infection of evil that originally came from the devil through Adam and Eve.  

If we only try to cut off the outward sins without allowing the Holy Spirit to deal with the root (pride, discontentment, self-will), we will keep struggling forever.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Why Does God Allow Satan and Evil to Exist?</span>

God could destroy Satan with one word. He could have done it long ago.  

Since an all-wise God has allowed Satan to exist, there must be a very good reason.

One major reason is that **evil serves a purpose** in testing and strengthening us.

> <div style="background-color:#D1ECF1; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #17A2B8; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#0C5460; font-weight:bold;">💡 Deep Insight</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#062C33;">
> Facing evil is like going to a gym. Muscles become strong only when they are subjected to resistance. A person becomes spiritually strong when he resists evil. If there is no resistance, there is no strength. Evil tests us to see whether we want God or evil, and it strengthens those who resist it.
> </span>
> </div>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Fire – A Good Servant but a Bad Master</span>

Is fire good or bad?  

Fire is a good servant but a bad master.  

- Controlled fire on a stove cooks our food.  
- Uncontrolled fire can burn down a house.  

Gold is purified by fire. Soap and water cannot remove the impurities — only fire can.  

In the same way, God allows evil (like fire) to test and purify us.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Scripture – Psalm 66:10-12</span>

> <span style="color:#276749;">“For You have tried us, O God; You have refined us as silver is refined. You brought us into the net; You laid an oppressive burden upon our loins. You made men ride over our heads; We went through fire and through water, yet You brought us out into a place of abundance.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Psalm 66:10-12</span>

God allows:
- Situations where we feel trapped (the net)  
- Oppressive burdens  
- People to “ride over our heads”  
- Fire and deep waters  

But the final result is that He brings us into a **place of abundance**.
