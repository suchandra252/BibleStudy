### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Introduction – Why We Must Understand Sin</span>

Welcome once again to **Words of Life**.

We continue the study of how we can come into a close connection with God.

There is not much preaching of sin in Christendom today. Many people’s idea of sin is exactly the same as the world’s idea of sin. They do not think that pride, seeking man’s honour, or selfishness are sins. Jesus spoke strongly against these things in Matthew 6.

A wrong idea of sin always produces a wrong idea of spirituality.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Jesus Came to Save Us From Sin</span>

The very first promise in the New Testament is this:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“She will bear a Son; and you shall call His name Jesus, for He will save His people from their sins.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Matthew 1:21</span>

The name **Jesus** means Saviour.

> <div style="background-color:#F8D7DA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #DC3545; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#721C24; font-weight:bold;">⚠️ Serious Warning</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#491217;">
> Many people preach that Jesus came to save us from poverty, from sickness, from earthly problems, or even just from hell. The Bible does not say that. The first promise is that He will save His people **from their sins**.
> </span>
> </div>

Most of our ideas of Christianity have come from our surroundings. A river that is polluted far downstream is not pure. Pure truth is found only at the source — the Word of God.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Religious vs Spiritual</span>

A religious person may read the Bible, pray, and attend church regularly, yet still not overcome sin.

True spirituality means being free from sin and free from idolatry.

In the Old Testament the Israelites were very religious, yet many of them worshipped idols. Elijah challenged them: choose between God and Baal.

Today the same choice remains: **Christ or the world**.

The world system is controlled by mammon (the love of money). Christ offers the grace of God through the Holy Spirit. We cannot serve both.

> <span style="color:#276749;">“Do not love the world nor the things in the world. If anyone loves the world, the love of the Father is not in him.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— 1 John 2:15</span>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Salvation From Sin and From This World System</span>

Jesus saves us from sin (as He commanded in the Great Commission).

He also saves us from this present evil world system:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“…who gave Himself for our sins so that He might rescue us from this present evil age…”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Galatians 1:4</span>

This is not the physical world of trees and mountains. It is the world system under the influence of the devil — called “the prince of this world” (John 14:30) and the one who controls the whole world (1 John 5:19).

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Areas Controlled by the World System</span>

The devil influences many areas of life:

- Entertainment and media that corrupt the mind
- Sports that can distract from God
- Education that promotes evolution and makes people foolish
- Fashion that is dictated by the world
- Medicine that often prioritises profit over true healing

Jesus has all authority in heaven and on earth (Matthew 28:18). Believers under His authority can swim against the current of the world. It requires constant alertness. Jesus is able to keep us from falling (Jude 24).

> <div style="background-color:#D1ECF1; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #17A2B8; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#0C5460; font-weight:bold;">💡 Deep Insight</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#062C33;">
> Beware of gospels that prioritise worldly things, and of preachers who are more interested in money than in souls. Jesus told the rich young ruler to sell his possessions, give to the poor, and follow Him — without depending on money. True preachers focus on the soul.
> </span>
> </div>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ What Jesus Actually Saves Us From</span>

Jesus saves us from:

- Love of money and the world (Luke 16)
- Worldly fashions, systems, and values
- Anger, bitterness, lust, pride, jealousy
- Fear of Satan or black magic
- Marital fights, temper, and selfishness

In their place He gives His own nature:

- Humility instead of pride
- Seeking God’s honour instead of man’s honour
- Seeking the kingdom instead of money
- Holiness instead of sinful pleasure

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ The Potter and the Clay</span>

> <span style="color:#276749;">“But now, O Lord, You are our Father; we are the clay, and You our potter.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Isaiah 64:8</span>

Jesus cleanses us and shapes us. When He asks us to remove “stones” from our lives (wrong relationships, habits, or loves), we must not resist. Those stones ruin the vessel He is making.

God never asks us to give up something that is truly good for us. Obeying Him leads to a glorious life.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Personal Encouragement</span>

Zac Poonen shares that after more than 45 years as a born-again Christian, his happiness has only increased through obedience. God is on our side against the devil, and He works for our good.

> <span style="color:#0B3D1B;">
