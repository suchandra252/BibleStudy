### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ From the Tenth Commandment to Sonship</span>

In the previous message we saw that under the law no one could keep the tenth commandment — “You shall not covet.” Under the New Covenant of grace, the Holy Spirit gives power to overcome inward desire for what God has not given.

Sin begins in the heart and then comes out through the mouth, eyes, hands, and body. True holiness is not external control (like self-discipline alone). It is the Holy Spirit changing the heart at the root.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ A New Relationship – Father and Son</span>

In the Old Testament almost no one could call God “Father” in a personal way. The relationship was mainly master and servant.

Jesus introduced something entirely new. He taught His disciples to pray:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“Our Father who is in heaven…”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Matthew 6:9</span>

When Jesus died, the curtain of the temple was torn from top to bottom (Matthew 27:51). This showed that the way into God’s presence is now open. When we enter, we do not meet a judge or a policeman. We meet a **Father** who wants sons.

> <div style="background-color:#D1ECF1; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #17A2B8; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#0C5460; font-weight:bold;">💡 Core Truth</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#062C33;">
> Being a son of God is far greater than being a servant of God. When we introduce our family, a son is greater than a servant. Many Christians have the idea reversed — they think the title “servant of God” is higher. That is a twisted view. God wants sons.
> </span>
> </div>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Why Jesus Came</span>

> <span style="color:#276749;">“For those whom He foreknew, He also predestined to become conformed to the image of His Son, so that He would be the firstborn among many brethren.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Romans 8:29</span>

God’s purpose is not only that we go to heaven. His purpose is that we become like Jesus, with Jesus as the eldest Brother among many brothers.

> <span style="color:#276749;">“But when the fullness of the time came, God sent forth His Son… so that we might receive the adoption as sons. Because you are sons, God has sent forth the Spirit of His Son into our hearts, crying, ‘Abba! Father!’ Therefore you are no longer a slave, but a son; and if a son, then an heir through God.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Galatians 4:4–7</span>

Old Testament servants could not cry “Abba, Father.” The Spirit of the Son now lives in us and enables us to do so. We are no longer slaves but sons and heirs.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ How a Son Differs from a Servant</span>

A servant works for wages, keeps fixed hours, and expects payment.  
A son does not work for wages. He shares in the Father’s business out of love and responsibility.

Sons inherit everything that belongs to the Father. Servants do not.

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #FFC107; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">💬 Key Distinction</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#533F03;">
> Sons have a sense of responsibility that servants and immature children do not have. At twelve years of age Jesus said, “Did you not know that I had to be in My Father’s house / about My Father’s business?” (Luke 2:49). Mature sons are concerned for the Father’s work and for His name to be glorified.
> </span>
> </div>

Immature believers act like children. They are mainly concerned with personal blessings — a bigger house, a better car, more comfort. Mature sons are concerned with the Father’s business.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ No Business Mentality with God</span>

There is a danger of doing business with God — serving in order to receive rewards, promotions, or material blessings.

Sons trust the Father to provide what they need without worry (Matthew 6:31–33). They seek first His kingdom and His righteousness.

Zac Poonen shares that for more than 45 years he has never done business with God. He serves as a son, trusting the Father’s wisdom even in the desires He gives.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Growing into Mature Sonship</span>

God wants us to grow from a child-like or servant attitude into the attitude of a mature son.

A mature son:
- Serves 24 hours a day out of love, not for fixed hours or bonuses
- Is concerned for the Father’s name and work
- Does not treat God as an employer
- Trusts the Father completely for every need
- Desires to become like the Elder Brother, Jesus

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">🙏 Prayer</span>

Heavenly Father,  

Thank You that through Jesus I am no longer a servant but a son. Thank You that the Spirit of Your Son lives in me and cries “Abba, Father.”

Forgive me for every time I have treated You like an employer or have been more concerned about my own blessings than about Your work.

Help me to grow into a mature son. Give me a sense of responsibility for Your business. Teach me to serve You 24 hours a day out of love, without expecting material bonuses.

I want to become like Your Son Jesus, the Elder Brother. Make me a true heir who trusts You completely.

In Jesus’ name, Amen.
