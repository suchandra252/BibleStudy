### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Introduction</span>

We have been looking at the origin of evil. The more we understand it, the more we can get rid of it in our own lives.

Satan has cleverly concealed the real root of evil. Most people (even Christians) only deal with the **fruit** of sin and therefore continue to have problems, because cutting off the fruit does not deal with the root.

Sin originated in:
- Pride  
- Discontentment  
- Rebellion against authority  
- Wanting one’s own way  

Lucifer did not commit murder, adultery or theft. He simply had wrong thoughts in his heart. In the same way, a person can do nothing wrong externally and still be like the devil inside.

True religion must purify us on the **inside**. That is impossible by our own strength — we need the life of God within us.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ God’s Purpose in Creating Man</span>

When God created the earth, He had a clear plan.  

He wanted to create people who would be His **representatives** (ambassadors) on this earth — just as a powerful country sends ambassadors to other nations.

Adam and Eve were created so that they could have children and form a new race of human beings who would manifest God’s nature on earth.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ The Easy Test in the Garden</span>

God placed Adam and Eve in the beautiful Garden of Eden filled with many good fruit trees.  

He gave them a very easy test:  
There were thousands of good trees, and only **one** tree from which they were not to eat.

If God had forbidden 10,000 trees and allowed only one, it would have been difficult. But He made it extremely easy — yet that is the very tree they went to, because the devil tempted them.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Why Did God Allow Temptation?</span>

God allowed Satan into the Garden because **without testing, a man cannot become holy**.

Adam was created **innocent**, not holy.  

No one can be created holy, because holiness requires a choice. If a person is created already holy, it means he had no choice in the matter (just as no one chooses the colour of their skin).

> <span style="color:#0C5460; font-weight:bold;">💡 Deep Insight</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#062C33;">
> Holiness is not something we are made with from the beginning. Holiness comes only when we choose the right thing. Without a choice, true holiness is impossible. Planets obey God 100%, yet they are not holy. Seeds obey the laws of nature 100%, yet they are not holy. Holiness can only exist where there is free choice.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Why Was the Forbidden Tree “Very Good”?</span>

In Genesis 1, after creating everything (including the forbidden tree), God said it was **very good**.

How could a forbidden tree be very good?  

Because through that tree Adam could be tempted, and through successfully resisting temptation he could become **holy**.  

Anything that makes it possible for a person to become holy is very good.

God could have made the tree ugly, smelly and full of thorns so that Adam would never go near it. But He made it the most attractive tree in the garden.  

Why?  
So that Adam’s choice would have real value. Choosing God over something ugly is not a great victory. Choosing God over something beautiful and desirable is true holiness.

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ The Heart of Every Temptation</span>

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #FFC107; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">💬 Core Truth</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#533F03;">
> Every temptation presents us with the same fundamental choice:  
> Will you choose the Creator, or will you choose the created thing?
> </span>
> </div>

The tree appealed to Eve in three ways (Genesis 3:6):
- Good for food (physical desire)  
- A delight to the eyes (visual attraction)  
- Desirable to make one wise (ambition / pride)

This is exactly how temptation comes to us today:
- An attractive woman → Will you lust or turn your eyes away?  
- Money / gold → Will you get it by wrong means or honour God?  
- Any beautiful created thing → Will you put it above the Creator?

God has made many created things in the world very attractive so that our choice of Him will be meaningful and costly.
