### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Sin Separates Us From God</span>

The only thing that creates distance between God and man is **sin**.

> <span style="color:#276749;">“But your iniquities have made a separation between you and your God, and your sins have hidden His face from you so that He does not hear.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Isaiah 59:2</span>

Even a small break stops the flow. Just as a one-millimetre gap in an electric wire stops the current, any sin (big or small) breaks living contact with God. God is light; sin is darkness. The two cannot mix. We must therefore rid our lives of all sin.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ First Remove the Guilt of Past Sin</span>

God cannot simply overlook past guilt because He loves us. Justice demands that sin be punished. The punishment for sin is eternal separation from God.

Christ took that punishment. On the cross the Father forsook Him for three hours. Jesus experienced, in concentrated form, what we would have experienced forever. That is why He cried, “My God, My God, why have You forsaken Me?” After the sin of the world was atoned for, fellowship was restored and He said, “Father, into Your hands I commit My spirit.”

The Christian faith stands on this foundation: Christ died for our sins and rose on the third day. If He did not rise, our faith is empty. Only Jesus died for the sins of the world and rose never to die again.

Other religions teach people to be good, love others, and help the poor. Christianity first says we are all guilty before God. Being good without dealing with past guilt is useless. God cannot pour His life into a vessel full of holes; the holes of guilt must first be closed.

> <div style="background-color:#F8D7DA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #DC3545; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;"> <span style="color:#721C24; font-weight:bold;">⚠️ Core Difference</span><br><br> <span style="color:#491217;"> Without the death and resurrection of Christ for our sins, Christianity has nothing unique. It becomes just another religion that says “do good and don’t harm others.” The foundation is that Jesus paid the price we could not pay, and He rose. </span> </div>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ The Superstructure – God’s Power</span>

Once the foundation is laid, Christianity does not stop at commandments. It says honestly: you cannot love God with all your heart or love your neighbour as yourself by your own strength.

The message is: “I will put My power in your heart so that you will be able to do these things.”

Telling a person to live a holy life without God’s power is like telling someone to run a fan, washing machine, or computer without electricity. It is impossible.

God’s power (grace) is greater than the power of sin.

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #FFC107; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;"> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">💬 Powerful Illustration</span><br><br> <span style="color:#533F03;"> The law of gravity pulls a plane down. The law of aerodynamics lifts it. As long as the plane obeys the laws of aerodynamics, it flies; if it disobeys, it crashes. Sin pulls us down like gravity. God’s grace lifts us like aerodynamics. Under grace, sin shall not have dominion over us (Romans 6). </span> </div>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Grace Is Always Sufficient</span>

> <span style="color:#276749;">“My grace is sufficient for you, for power is perfected in weakness.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— 2 Corinthians 12:9</span>

Grace matches the need. If the trial is small, the grace is enough. If the trial is great, the grace is greater. God also limits the temptation:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“No temptation has overtaken you but such as is common to man; and God is faithful, who will not allow you to be tempted beyond what you are able, but with the temptation will provide the way of escape also, so that you will be able to endure it.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— 1 Corinthians 10:13</span>

The way of escape is His grace.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ How to Receive the Power</span>

Electricity does not come automatically; we must apply for it. In the same way, we must ask the Father for the power of the Holy Spirit.

Many Christians live without this power, just as some people live without electricity. Sensible people ask for what they need.

In the Old Testament God gave commandments but no inward power to keep them. For 1,500 years Israel proved that no one can reach God’s standard by human effort. In the New Covenant God writes His law on the heart and gives the Holy Spirit inside so that we can keep it.

> <span style="color:#276749;">“If you love Me, you will keep My commandments. I will ask the Father, and He will give you another Helper…”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— John 14:15–16</span>

The main reason the Holy Spirit is given is to help us obey God, not merely to give gifts.
