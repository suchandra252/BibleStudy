### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Introduction</span>

This message continues from *Sin and Salvation*.  

Sin entered the human race not merely as an act of disobedience, but as **disobedience that came from unbelief**. Eve first lost faith in God’s word and in God’s love — and only then did she disobey.

In the New Testament, God looks for the **obedience of faith**.

> <span style="color:#276749;">“…the obedience of faith…”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Romans 1:5; Romans 16:26</span>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Two Kinds of Obedience</span>

There is a big difference between:

1. Obedience that comes from **fear** or from a desire for **reward**  
2. Obedience that comes from **faith** in God’s love

> <div style="background-color:#F8D7DA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #DC3545; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#721C24; font-weight:bold;">⚠️ Warning About False Obedience</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#491217;">
> Many people tithe or obey certain commands in order to get financial blessing. That is not the obedience of a child. That is doing business with God — like putting money in a bank to get interest. Would you treat your own father like that? God wants a relationship, not a transaction.
> </span>
> </div>

Jesus said:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“If you love Me, you will keep My commandments.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— John 14:15</span>

True obedience flows from love, not from the hope of profit.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Mechanical Obedience Does Not Please God</span>

God loves a cheerful giver and a cheerful obey-er.

> <span style="color:#276749;">“God loves a cheerful giver.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— 2 Corinthians 9:7</span>

Coming to meetings only as a routine, without joy, is not the obedience God desires.  

Even accidental weakness (for example, losing one’s temper in a moment) is different from the deeper sin of **unbelief** — choosing not to trust God.

> <span style="color:#276749;">“…whatever is not from faith is sin.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Romans 14:23</span>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Faith Is Higher Than the Senses and Reason</span>

Satan’s first question was:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“Has God really said…?”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Genesis 3:1</span>

He still uses the same method today — making us question God’s word because our eyes, feelings, or reasoning say something different.

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #FFC107; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">💬 Powerful Illustration</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#533F03;">
> Two boys look at the sun. The older boy says, “The sun is moving. I can see it.” The younger boy believes his father, who told him that the earth is rotating. The younger boy is right. Our senses often deceive us. The earth is spinning at more than 1,000 km per hour, yet we do not feel it. Dogs can hear sounds we cannot hear. Police dogs can smell what we cannot smell. Sight and feeling also mislead us.
> </span>
> </div>

Science keeps changing its conclusions. God’s Word does not.

> <span style="color:#276749;">“Heaven and earth will pass away, but My words will not pass away.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Matthew 24:35</span>

Reason is like a small torch — it lights only a little area. Faith is like a bright lamp — it shows the whole path because it trusts what God has said.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ What Faith Really Means</span>

Faith is not just agreeing with a doctrine in the mind.  

Faith is **total trust** — like putting your money in a bank, or like giving your life to a person in marriage.

If we really trust that God loves us perfectly, we will obey His commands the way a sick person takes medicine from a trusted doctor — knowing it is for our good.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ When God Says “No”</span>

God’s answers to prayer can be compared to traffic lights:
- **Red** — No (often because the thing would harm us)
- **Orange** — Wait
- **Green** — Yes

The devil uses a “No” to whisper, “See, God does not love you.”  

But a father who refuses to give a knife to a small child is acting in love, not in cruelty. Eve sinned because she believed the lie that God was withholding something good.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Key Takeaways</span>

> <div style="background-color:#D4EDDA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #28A745; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#155724; font-weight:bold;">✅ Summary of Main Truths</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#0B3D1B;">
> • Eve’s fall was not only disobedience — it was disobedience that came from lost faith in God’s love.<br>
> • God wants the “obedience of faith,” not obedience from fear or from desire for reward.<br>
> • Doing spiritual things to get material blessing is treating God like a bank, not like a Father.<br>
> • Whatever is not from faith is sin (Romans 14:23).<br>
> • Our senses and reason can deceive us; God’s Word does not change.<br>
> • Faith is total trust, not mere mental agreement.<br>
> • When God says “No,” it is often because He loves us too much to give us what would harm us.<br>
> • Cheerful obedience that trusts God’s goodness is the obedience that pleases Him.
> </span>
> </div>
