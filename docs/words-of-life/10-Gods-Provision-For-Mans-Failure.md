### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Recap</span>

In the last message we saw how man fell into sin (Genesis 3).  

Adam and Eve hid, covered themselves with fig leaves, and blamed others. Today we look at **God’s response** to that failure — and the provision He made.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Fear Entered Through Sin</span>

When God called, Adam said:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“I heard the sound of You in the garden, and I was afraid because I was naked; so I hid myself.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Genesis 3:10</span>

This is the **first mention of fear** in the Bible. Fear came in through sin.

> <div style="background-color:#D1ECF1; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #17A2B8; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#0C5460; font-weight:bold;">💡 Deep Insight</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#062C33;">
> Sin is quicker than potassium cyanide. As soon as it entered Adam, it made him afraid, made him hide, and made him blame others. When we are saved from sin, we are also saved from this fear of God as an enemy.
> </span>
> </div>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ God Solved the Problem Before He Rebuked</span>

God did not first scold Adam and Eve.  

He first spoke to the **serpent** and announced the solution.

> <span style="color:#276749;">“I will put enmity between you and the woman, and between your seed and her seed; He shall bruise you on the head, and you shall bruise him on the heel.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Genesis 3:15</span>

This is the **first gospel message** in the Bible.

- The “seed of the woman” is Christ.
- Satan would bruise Christ’s heel (the cross — real suffering, but not final defeat).
- Christ would crush Satan’s head (complete victory).

> <div style="background-color:#FFF3CD; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #FFC107; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#856404; font-weight:bold;">💬 Key Quote</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#533F03;">
> God is not here first of all to rebuke me. He first wants to solve the problem and assure me that the problem is solved. Then He deals with my life.
> </span>
> </div>

This is still God’s way: **mercy first, then discipline**.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ The Consequences That Followed</span>

**To the woman (Genesis 3:16):**
- Pain in childbirth
- Desire for her husband
- In the fallen world, the husband would rule over her

**To Adam (Genesis 3:17–19):**
- God did **not** curse Adam personally.
- He cursed the **ground**.
- Toil, thorns, sweat, and finally death: “Dust you are, and to dust you shall return.”

God still distinguished between the person and the consequence. He judged the sin, but He also prepared redemption.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Fig Leaves vs Garments of Skin</span>

Adam and Eve covered themselves with fig leaves — a picture of **human righteousness**.

Leaves wither. Human efforts to look good before God cannot last.

Then God Himself acted:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“The Lord God made garments of skin for Adam and his wife, and clothed them.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Genesis 3:21</span>

An animal had to die. Blood was shed. That is the first picture of **substitution** — an innocent life given so that the guilty could be covered.

> <div style="background-color:#D4EDDA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #28A745; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#155724; font-weight:bold;">✅ Important Truth</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#0B3D1B;">
> • Fig leaves = our own efforts, zeal, giving, religious activity — they cannot cover sin.<br>
> • Garments of skin = God’s provision through the death of another — fulfilled in Christ.<br>
> • We must throw away our human righteousness when we come before God and receive the covering He provides.
> </span>
> </div>

God knew their need and provided. He knows your need too, and He will do for you more than you can think.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Driven Out — One Sin Was Enough</span>

God drove them out of Eden so that they would not eat from the tree of life in a fallen state and live forever as sinners.

> <span style="color:#276749;">“So He drove the man out; and at the east of the garden of Eden He stationed the cherubim and the flaming sword which turned every direction to guard the way to the tree of life.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Genesis 3:24</span>

**One sin** was enough to shut them out of Eden. That shows how serious sin is.

The flaming sword that guards the way to life is a picture of the **cross**. The way back to the tree of life is not a shortcut spiritual experience. The sword must fall — we must be crucified with Christ. Only then is the way open.

Satan hides the consequences of sin. We must not be ignorant of his schemes.

> <span style="color:#276749;">“…so that no advantage would be taken of us by Satan, for we are not ignorant of his schemes.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— 2 Corinthians 2:11</span>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ A Warning About Voices Close to Us</span>

Adam’s fall included this: he listened to his wife instead of listening to God.

This is not a word against marriage. It is a word about **priority**.

No human voice — husband, wife, parent, or friend — must be allowed to drown out God’s voice. Satan often works through someone close, as we saw in the previous message.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ What Sin Really Is</span>

Sin is not only a bad action. In this story sin is:
- Rebellion against God’s clear word
- Doubting God’s love
- Choosing a human desire above God’s will
- Then hiding, covering, and blaming

That same pattern still works in us.

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Key Takeaways</span>

> <div style="background-color:#D4EDDA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #28A745; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#155724; font-weight:bold;">✅ Summary of Main Truths</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#0B3D1B;">
> • Fear entered the human race through sin. Salvation from sin also saves us from that fear.<br>
> • God first announced the gospel (Genesis 3:15) before He announced the consequences.<br>
> • Christ would be wounded, but Satan would be crushed.<br>
> • God cursed the ground, not Adam personally — judgment and mercy together.<br>
> • Fig leaves are human righteousness. They wither. They cannot cover us before God.<br>
> • Garments of skin required a death. They point to the cross and to Christ’s righteousness.<br>
> • One sin was enough to close Eden. Sin is never small.<br>
> • The flaming sword pictures the cross — the way back to life is death to self with Christ.<br>
> • Do not hide from God, blame others, or let any human voice override God’s voice.<br>
> • God knows your need and provides more than you can think.
> </span>
> </div>

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">🙏 Prayer</span>

Heavenly Father,

I throw away my fig leaves — my own efforts to look righteous. Clothe me with the righteousness of Christ.

Save me from the fear that came through sin. Save me from hiding and from blaming others. Help me never to let any human voice become louder than Yours.
