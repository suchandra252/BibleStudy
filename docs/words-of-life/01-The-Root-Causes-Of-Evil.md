### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Introduction</span>

Today we begin a new series titled **Words of Life**.

All of our problems are basically caused by the fact that our connection with God has been broken.

God is the source of all life. What we call life on earth — the life of trees, animals and human beings — is only created life. But what God Himself has is **eternal life**. That is the life God wants us to have.

> <div style="background-color:#D1ECF1; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #17A2B8; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#0C5460; font-weight:bold;">💡 Deep Insight</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#062C33;">
> The Bible is not just a book of religion or a book that teaches us about a particular faith. It is a book that shows us how we can partake of God’s own eternal life. It tells us how God created the first man and woman, and even before that, how evil came into this world.
> </span>
> </div>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Why Is There So Much Evil in the World?</span>

We know that the world is full of evil. Many people suffer tremendously because of it. So many people do evil to others.

A very important question arises:  
**Where did all this evil originate?**

Did somebody have to teach people to do evil?

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Evil Is Already in Human Nature</span>

Think about a small child born into your home.

- Do you have to teach that child to fight or quarrel?  
- Do you have to teach the child to grab things for himself?  
- Do you have to teach the child how to tell lies?  

**No.**

In fact, we have to teach our children how to speak the truth, how to be unselfish, and how to love others.

> <div style="background-color:#F8D7DA; padding:12px 16px; border-left:5px solid #DC3545; border-radius:5px; margin:15px 0;">
> <span style="color:#721C24; font-weight:bold;">⚠️ Serious Observation</span><br><br>
> <span style="color:#491217;">
> Selfishness, lying, fighting and hating are already in the nature of every child. Evil comes with the child into the world. As the child grows up, that evil grows and matures until he can become a very evil person. This is true regardless of which religion the child is born into.
> </span>
> </div>

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Is the Devil More Powerful Than God?</span>

When we see so much evil in the world, some people begin to think that the devil is even more powerful than God. That is why most people remain in evil and very few come out into real goodness.

Important questions arise in the mind:

- Why did God permit evil to come into this world?  
- Why doesn’t God control it?  
- If there is a source of evil called the devil, why doesn’t God finish him off?  
- Isn’t God more powerful than the devil?  

If a terrorist is doing a lot of harm to a country, the government will try to finish him off. Why doesn’t God destroy the devil who has done far worse than any terrorist?

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ God Is Pure Love — No Darkness in Him</span>

The Bible clearly teaches:

- God is a good God.  
- There is **no darkness** in Him.  
- There is **no evil** in Him.  
- There is **no hatred** in Him.  

> <span style="color:#276749;">“God is light, and in Him is no darkness at all.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— 1 John 1:5</span>

God is perfect love from beginning to end. There is not even an atom of hatred in Him.

So the question remains:  
**How then did evil come into this world?**

---

### <span style="color:#1A5276; background-color:#EAF2F8; padding:5px 12px; border-radius:6px;">✨ Something Happened Between Genesis 1:1 and 1:2</span>

The Bible begins:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Genesis 1:1</span>

But the very next verse says:

> <span style="color:#276749;">“The earth was formless and void, and darkness was over the surface of the deep…”</span>  
> <span style="color:#276749;">— Genesis 1:2</span>

God never creates anything dark.  
God never creates anything empty or formless.

Something must have happened **between** verse 1 and verse 2.  

That “something” is the origin of evil — the fall of Lucifer (who later became Satan).
